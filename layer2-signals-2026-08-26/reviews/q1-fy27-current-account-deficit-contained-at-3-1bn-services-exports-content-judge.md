- SEO/AEO audit: every blog needs a clear keyword, answer target, and internal link opportunity.

- Thread: every post must survive being quoted out of context.
- Carousel: compression-distortion lens; slides must not collapse nuanced claims into misleading statements.
- Blog: highest nuance standard; every strong claim needs support.

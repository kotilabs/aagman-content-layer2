everyone is quoting the milestone. the milestone is wrong in an instructive way. us debt held by the public stood at 2.27 trillion on august 24, against a 2.48 trillion economy: 99.4 percent of gdp, not the 100 being claimed. total debt, 0.04 trillion, crossed that line years ago. which "debt" you quote moves the answer by 23 points.

the number that deserves the attention is the bill. net interest hit a ,247 billion annual rate in the second quarter, a record. one dollar in six of federal spending. nearly 40 cents of every revenue dollar. more than the entire defense budget. and it compounds mechanically: every maturing 2020-21 coupon gets refinanced at 3.31 to 3.76 percent, so the line rises even if the deficit does nothing.

the underreported detail: the last time interest consumed this share of gdp, the early 1990s, it forced actual fiscal consolidation. the deficit behind today's share is 5.8 percent of gdp with no recession to justify it.

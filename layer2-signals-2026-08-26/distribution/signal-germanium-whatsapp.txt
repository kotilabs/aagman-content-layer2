china has been slowing customs clearance of germanium and quartz shipments to taiwan since 2025, per nikkei asia on august 20. no ban was announced. clearance just got slower.

the numbers:
- chinese domestic germanium: *$3,104/kg*, down *9.2%* in august
- us warehouse germanium: *$6,350/kg*, holding firm
- western premium over china: *104.6%*, up from *82.9%* in july
- global supply: *130-140 tonnes a year*, *80%* in china, all a byproduct of zinc smelting

why it works: every export needs a license since 2023, and customs speed is the second dial, deniable and reversible. verified impact: optical makers, chip equipment (quartz lead times up by months), aerospace magnets.

takeaway: the chokepoint moved from prices to lead times. nobody has shown it reaching wafer output. the loudest fab claim came from a trading desk short tsmc.

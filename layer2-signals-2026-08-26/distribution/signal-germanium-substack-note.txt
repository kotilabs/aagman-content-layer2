the most interesting part of the germanium story is what did not happen. no export ban, no license denial, no announcement. since 2025, chinese customs has simply been slower with taiwan-bound shipments of germanium and quartz, and by august that was enough to extend lead times across taiwanese optics, chip equipment, and aerospace supply lines.

hold the price data next to it. chinese domestic germanium fell 9.2 percent in august, giving back most of july's panic spike. american warehouse germanium held at ,350 a kilo. the western premium widened to 104.6 percent. the squeeze, in other words, is in physical availability outside china, not in the headline price. availability is the instrument.

the underreported detail: the claim that this is already hitting taiwan's wafer fabs traces to a single trading desk note that disclosed a short position in tsmc. the verified record stops at lead times.

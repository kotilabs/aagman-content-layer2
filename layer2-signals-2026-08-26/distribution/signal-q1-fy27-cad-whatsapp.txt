india's april-june current account deficit came in at *$3.1bn*, nearly flat to a year ago. the goods trade deficit widened by *$16.8bn* to *$85.7bn*. that gap is the story.

the numbers:
- net services exports: *+$52.2bn*, covering *61%* of the goods deficit
- remittances: *+$41.4bn*, up *34%* in a year
- services plus remittances covered *109%* of the goods hole
- capital account: *-$5.0bn* as foreign investors pulled *$9.6bn*
- overall balance of payments: *-$8.1bn*, drawn from reserves

why it holds: the rbi's june swap window absorbed the full hedging cost on nri dollar deposits. *$72.8bn* arrived in eleven weeks; the window closed a month early. those deposits come due between 2029 and 2031.

takeaway: the headline deficit is the residual of one export sector, one remittance spike, and a central bank renting out its balance sheet.

## Recommended concepts (if any)

### Concept 1: "The 104.6% Split"
- Central insight: germanium now has two prices — $3,104 inside China and $6,350 in a US warehouse — and the widening gap (82.9% → 104.6% in one month) is the chokepoint measured better than any headline price.
- Data: both price levels (Aug 3, 2026), both premium readings (Jul vs Aug), domestic July +27.8% and August -9.2%.
- Visualization type: divergence chart — two price lines (domestic vs US warehouse) Jan 2025–Aug 2026 with the premium annotated at both ends.
- Composition notes: eye follows the domestic line spiking then falling while the Western line holds flat and high; the shaded gap between them is the story. Annotation: "The squeeze is availability, not price."
- Sources: SMM via rare-earth-mining.com (2026-08-03).

### Concept 2: "The Escalation Ladder"
- Central insight: Beijing's materials leverage has moved down a ladder of deniability — from a global license rule (2023), to destination-specific denial (US, Dec 2024), to informal customs slow-walk (Taiwan, 2025–26) — each step harder to litigate than the last.
- Data: the three dated regime events; shipment impact (~-80% US vs 2023, snippet-labeled); delay onset "since 2025"; lead-time extension "months" (equipment).
- Visualization type: timeline with a deniability axis.
- Composition notes: left to right, three stamped events; above each, what was formal; below, what it cost targets. Payoff: the latest step has no document to challenge.
- Sources: Taiwan News/Nikkei (2026-08-21); USGS via Metal Tech News (snippet-labeled); rare-earth-mining.com policy coverage.

### Concept 3: "Where the Delay Actually Bites"
- Central insight: the verified impact map is optics, chip equipment (quartz), and aerospace magnets — not wafer fabs; the largest circulating claim is the one without evidence.
- Data: the three verified impact zones with their mechanisms (germanium→infrared/fiber, quartz→equipment, magnets→aerospace); the zero-confirmation status of fab output; TSG's disclosed short-TSM positioning as the claim's origin.
- Visualization type: flow/map diagram — material → verified affected industry → unverified extension (grayed, stamped "no confirmation").

- Sources: Nikkei via Taiwan News (2026-08-21); Trading Strategy Guides (2026-08-23).

### Concept 4: "130 Tonnes, No Mine"
- Central insight: germanium supply is a ~130–140 t/yr byproduct stream, ~80% Chinese, that cannot respond to price — which is why a licensing dial works at all.

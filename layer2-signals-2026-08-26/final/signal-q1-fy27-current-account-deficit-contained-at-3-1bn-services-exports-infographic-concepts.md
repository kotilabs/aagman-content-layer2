## Recommended concepts (if any)

### Concept 1: "The $3.1bn Residual"
- Central insight: India's contained CAD is the leftover of two flows covering 109% of an $85.7bn goods deficit — remove either and the quarter prints a very large deficit.
- Data: goods -$85.7bn; services +$52.2bn (61% coverage); transfers +$41.4bn (to 109%); income -$11.1bn; net -$3.1bn. All RBI PR 2026-27/894, Q1 FY27, USD bn.
- Visualization type: waterfall / bridge chart.
- Composition notes: eye starts at the tall red goods bar, descends through two green offset bars of visibly unequal size, lands on the tiny residual. Payoff: the residual's smallness is revealed as arithmetic, not health. Side annotation: "61% of the offset is one sector."
- Sources: RBI PR 2026-27/894.

### Concept 2: "One Window, $72.8 Billion"
- Central insight: The June swap facility pulled $72.85bn in 11 weeks — 2.5x the entire 2013 FCNR(B) window — and 90% of it came through the most subsidised retail channel.
- Data: cumulative inflow curve ($20.72bn Jul 17 → $56.85bn Aug 13 → $72.85bn Aug 21); channel split FCNR(B) $65.40bn / OFCB $4.86bn / ECB $2.59bn; 2013 comparison ~$26bn in 3 months. Tenor note: liabilities due 2029–31, swaps non-cancellable, RBI carries FX risk.
- Visualization type: timeline with stacked channel bars + a 2013-vs-2026 comparison panel.
- Composition notes: left panel the steep 2026 curve, right panel the flat 2013 bar for scale; channel split as a proportional bar under the 2026 total. Payoff: the early-closure stamp (Aug 31) reads as "demand was for the subsidy."
- Sources: RBI PR 2026-27/900, 2026-27/948; MUFG Research 2026-06-08; ETV Bharat/PTI 2026-07-20.

### Concept 3: "Two Quarters, Two Stories" (current vs capital account divergence)
- Central insight: The same quarter that printed a contained CAD also printed an $8.1bn reserve drawdown and a $9.6bn FPI outflow — containment and stress coexisted on opposite sides of the ledger.
- Data: current account -$3.1bn vs capital account -$5.0bn; FPI -$9.6bn vs FDI +$7.8bn; BoP -$8.1bn; June-only +$2.9bn turnaround. Post-June: FPI +$4.2bn Jul, +$2.3bn Aug 1–21; reserves ~$707bn Aug 7.
- Visualization type: mirrored bar comparison (current vs capital account, Q1 FY26 vs Q1 FY27) with a small June reversal callout.
- Composition notes: two columns of paired bars; the eye follows the FPI bar's deep red, then the June green callout breaks the pattern. Payoff: the "contained" headline and the drawdown are the same quarter.
- Sources: RBI PR 2026-27/894; BusinessLine 2026-08-25 (labelled secondary); Financial Express 2026-08-14.

### Concept 4: "The Remittance Anomaly"
- Central insight: Net transfers jumped 34% YoY — three to six times trend — precisely when the rupee fell 7%, making the biggest offset in the CAD also the most cyclical.
- Data: transfers $30.9bn → $41.4bn (+34%); trend ~5–10%; INR ~7% YTD depreciation; USD/INR 95.7143 (Aug 25).
- Visualization type: divergence — transfers growth vs INR level over the past year.
- Composition notes: transfers bar towers over prior-year quarters; INR line slopes down behind it. Payoff annotation: "This hedge strengthens when the currency weakens. What happens if INR holds 95?"
- Sources: RBI PR 2026-27/894; RBI FBIL ticker 2026-08-25; Sabnavis via FE 2026-08-14.

## What to avoid

- "Brent ~$92" oil-price panel: the precise level is unverified (sources show ~$88 Aug 11, >$100 in June). A range band could work inside Concept 1's annotation but does not carry its own graphic. Dropped.
- FY27 forecast visual (SBI: BoP +$5–10bn, CAD 1.5–1.7% of GDP): a single-house estimate; visualizing it would launder a forecast into a fact. Dropped.
- Sector winners/losers board (IT, banks, OMCs): implication lists are not data; violates the no-advice posture. Dropped.
- -$10.1bn errors & omissions residual: real but unexplained; a chart of an unexplained number invites speculation. Monitor only.

## Final recommendation

Proceed with Concepts 1 and 2 (both fully primary-sourced). Concept 3 is strong but requires labelling the July/Aug FPI figures as secondary. Concept 4 is a viable companion if the divergence framing is wanted. Hold nothing on data grounds — the core visuals rest on RBI primary releases.

## Recommended concepts

### Concept 1: The scorecard — fewer losers, losing more
- **Central insight:** SEBI's FY26 data shows the reset protected people by removing them: aggregate retail losses fell 18% while the average remaining trader lost 2% more, and 87.7% still lost.
- **Data to include:**
  - Active individual traders: **10.62 mn → 8.75 mn**, first decline since FY16; **4.6 mn** exited (SEBI via Informist).
  - Aggregate net losses: **-18% to ₹91,685 cr**; average loss per trader: **₹1.13 lakh → ₹1.17 lakh** (SEBI via Informist).
  - Loss-making share: **90.9% → 87.7%**; options = **92%** of losses (SEBI via Informist).
- **Visualization type:** Paired diverging bars / before-after panel.
- **Composition notes:** Two columns, FY25 and FY26. Four rows: traders, aggregate losses, loss per trader, loss share. Arrows on each row; three point down, one points up (loss per trader) — that single red up-arrow in a field of green down-arrows is the payoff. Footnote: "protection by subtraction."
- **Sources:** SEBI FY26 profitability analysis via Informist, 20 Aug 2026 (https://informistmedia.com/EquityWire/58150/Sebi-Analysis-Share-of-individuals-losing-money-in-equity-F-O-88-37-FY26-vs-91-37-FY25-says-SEBI).

### Concept 2: Who's left — the concentration map
- **Central insight:** The reset didn't democratize the market; it moved 92% of turnover into the hands of the 23% of traders with serious capital, and 85% of options activity into ten prop desks.
- **Data to include:**
  - **77%** of traders (<₹1 lakh margin) = **8% of turnover / 14% of losses**; **23%** (>₹1 lakh) = **92% of turnover / 86% of losses** (SEBI sample report via Informist — label "indicative sample").
  - Top-10 props: **~75% of prop gross profit**, **~85% of options activity**, 65% of futures; **99%** of FPI/prop profits involved an algo (SEBI via Informist).
  - FY26 gross profits: prop **-3%**, FPI **-55%**, MF **-54%** (SEBI via Informist).
- **Visualization type:** Marimekko / proportional area chart plus a concentration donut.
- **Composition notes:** Left: a wide rectangle split horizontally — the thin 77% band labelled "8% of turnover," the thick 23% band labelled "92%." Right: a donut where a 10-entity slice dominates. Below, three small profit-change chips: prop -3%, FPI -55%, MF -54%. Payoff: the small band owns the market.
- **Sources:** SEBI via Informist, 20 Aug 2026 (same URL as above). The 77/23 split must carry the "5,050-trader indicative sample" caveat in the footnote.

### Concept 3: The rule stack — a two-year squeeze in one timeline
- **Central insight:** India's options collapse was engineered by six dated levers, each raising capital or cost per ticket — and the tape responded on schedule.
- **Data to include:**
  - Nov 2024: contract size **₹5–10L → ₹15–20L**; Nifty lot **25 → 75**. Feb 2025: **upfront premium** (1st) and **expiry-day calendar-spread margin revoked** (10th). FY25–26: **intraday monitoring**, delta-based OI, MWPL tied to free float. 1 Apr 2026: **STT futures 0.02%→0.05%, options 0.1%→0.15%** (Pocketful / Tradeify / Zerodha).
  - Volume response: India contracts **16 bn → 4 bn** Oct 2024 → Mar 2025 (FIA); options volumes **-51% FY26** (SEBI via ET); **₹34.48 lakh crore** Aug 2026, lowest since Nov 2023 (ET).
- **Visualization type:** Dual-track timeline — rules on top, volume line below.
- **Composition notes:** Horizontal axis Oct 2024 → Aug 2026. Top track stamps each rule as a flag at its effective date. Bottom track plots the contract-count/turnover decline. The eye follows the flags down to the falling line; the Aug 2026 endpoint is annotated with the 21-month-low callout. Payoff: cause and effect on one axis.
- **Sources:** Pocketful (https://www.pocketful.in/blog/trading/sebi-fo-new-rules/), Tradeify (https://tradeify.co/post/sebi-fo-new-rules-2026-us-futures-alternative), FIA (https://www.fia.org/marketvoice/articles/indias-options-crackdown-weighs-global-etd-trading-volume), ET (2 Sep 2026 and 6 Aug 2026, URLs above).

### Concept 4: The duopoly flip — BSE's fee-priced coup
- **Central insight:** When volume became scarce, it became contestable: BSE took 55.4% of notional F&O turnover in April 2026 by pricing options at a seventh of NSE's fee and futures at zero — but NSE kept two-thirds of the premium pool.
- **Data to include:**
  - Notional F&O share: BSE **55.4%** vs NSE **44.6%** (Apr 2026; from 43.6% in March) (Trade Brains).
  - Fees: BSE futures **0**, options **0.005%**; NSE futures **0.00183%**, options **0.0355%** (Trade Brains).
  - NSE retains **~66%** of options premium turnover (Trade Brains); NSE options fee growth **+2% vs +92%** (Bloomberg via FIA).
- **Visualization type:** Crossing slope chart plus fee comparison bars.
- **Composition notes:** Top: two lines cross at April 2026 — BSE ascending, NSE descending. Bottom: a fee bar pair where NSE's options bar towers 7× over BSE's, and BSE's futures bar is absent entirely. A footnote strip carries the premium-share caveat. Payoff: the crossing and the 7× fee gap explain each other.
- **Sources:** Trade Brains (https://tradebrains.in/fo-market-is-bse-the-new-preference-for-fo-traders/), FIA (URL above).

### Concept 5: Where did the risk go — the displacement ledger
- **Central insight:** The policy assumed suppressed speculation would become domestic ownership; the ledger shows record SIPs on one side and an unmeasured offshore-futures leak on the other.
- **Data to include:**
  - SIP contributions: record **₹32,087 cr (Mar 2026)**, ₹31,961 cr (Jul 2026); SIP AUM **20.57%** of industry AUM, all-time high; stoppage ratio **>100%** in Mar–Apr (Finnovate / Cafemutual).
  - Cash-market delivery-to-traded ratio up to **~30%** (SEBI annual report via ET).
  - Offshore side: US prop firms pitching Indian traders evaluation accounts from **<$100**, "FEMA grey zone" per the vendor itself; **no measured flow exists** (Tradeify, 1 Aug 2026 — label as vendor material).
- **Visualization type:** Balance-scale / split-flow diagram.
- **Composition notes:** A single outflow node ("4.6 mn exited F&O in FY26") splits into two branches: left, "SIPs & delivery" with the record SIP bar; right, "offshore US futures" rendered deliberately as a dashed, unquantified arrow — the missing number is the point. A question mark sits on the right branch. Payoff: the visible half is healthy; the invisible half is unmeasured.
- **Sources:** Finnovate (https://www.finnovate.in/learn/blog/sip-stoppage-ratio-stability-stagnation-2026), Cafemutual (https://cafemutual.com/news/industry/38488-amfi-monthly-sip-inflows-almost-touch-rs-32000-crore-equity-inflows-decline-by-15-in-july-2026), ET 6 Aug 2026 (URL above), Tradeify (URL above; vendor-labelled).

## What to avoid

- A "₹34.48 lakh crore vs ₹200 lakh crore" comparison — the CNBCTV18 figure uses an unreconciled metric and its page 404s; do not mix series.
- BSE's August turnover as "₹32.2 crore" — ET's printed unit is a misprint; either use "lowest since June 2025" qualitatively or omit the level.
- The CY24 ₹490 trn → CY25 ₹391 trn turnover pair as a lead visual — it is a broker blog's second-hand figure; usable only as background texture in Concept 3 with attribution.
- Any chart implying the offshore US-futures migration is large — the only evidence is vendor marketing. The honest visual (Concept 5) makes its unmeasured status the feature.
- Korea analogue as a headline chart — some volume figures are snippet-level from Korean-language journal data; keep it to a small perspective panel if used at all.
- Rounding the loss asymmetry ("losses down, losses per trader up") into "things improved" — that is the one misreading this signal punishes.

## Final recommendation

Proceed with **Concepts 1–5**. Lead with **Concept 1** (the scorecard) — it carries the signal's central tension in one glance and every number is regulator-grade. Pair it with **Concept 3** (the rule-stack timeline) if two are commissioned, since causation and effect on one axis is the strongest standalone narrative. Concepts 2, 4 and 5 each support a distinct slide of the carousel decks and can be commissioned as a set.

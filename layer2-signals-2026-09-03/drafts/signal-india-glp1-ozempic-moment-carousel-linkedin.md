# Carousel: India's Ozempic Moment, Six Months In

## Slide 1
- **Kicker:** Patent cliff
- **Headline:** On 20 March, India's semaglutide patent expired. Generics opened 85% below the branded price.
- **Body:** Thirteen companies launched 26 brands in the first week. Fifty-plus within a month. India became the world's first live test of a generic Ozempic market.
- **Hero stat:** ₹1,290/month (Natco vial) vs ₹8,800–11,175 for branded Ozempic — Fierce Pharma / ET, Mar 2026
- **Design note:** Price ladder graphic — branded price at top, ₹1,290 at bottom, with the date as the hinge.
- **Speaker note:** Hook with the cliff itself — one date, one collapse, no preamble.

## Slide 2
- **Kicker:** The surprise
- **Headline:** The innovator did not get destroyed. It cut price — and sold more.
- **Body:** Novo cut Ozempic and Wegovy starters 36–48% to ₹5,660 on 1 April. In April, Novo's India volumes rose 40%. The whole market grew 56% month-on-month to 414,000 units.
- **Hero stat:** 414,000 units in April, +56% m/m — Pharmarack via Bloomberg
- **Design note:** Two rising bars — total market and Novo units — side by side, both labelled.
- **Speaker note:** Break the expected script early: expansion, not erosion.

## Slide 3
- **Kicker:** Sold out
- **Headline:** Only 3–4 of the first 10 brands were still in stock online within days.
- **Body:** Jefferies blamed bad demand forecasts, thin online allocation, and a supply chain snarled by a regulatory crackdown on unauthorised sales — in a market with ~5% penetration of diabetics and ~4% of the obese.
- **Hero stat:** ~5% GLP-1 penetration among diabetics — Jefferies via ET, 27 Mar 2026
- **Design note:** Shelf of ten product boxes, six greyed out with "sold out" tags.
- **Speaker note:** Establish that supply, not demand, was the binding constraint.

## Slide 4
- **Kicker:** The input
- **Headline:** The raw material fell from $900 a gram to $90–160. One approved supplier ships it. It sits in Hangzhou.
- **Body:** Chinese multi-tonne capacity outpaced demand before the patents even lapsed; recombinant variants trade near $50–60/gram, with another 20–30% softening expected (ET, Apr 2026). The cheap molecule enters India through a single door: Sinopep-Allsino, the only DCGI-registered API source. Domestic entrants — MSN, Apothecon — are not registered at scale yet.
- **Hero stat:** API -85% ($900/g → $90–160/g); 1 registered supplier — Economic Times, 17 Apr 2026
- **Design note:** Split slide — falling line chart on the left, one supply route drawn from Hangzhou into India on the right, marked "only registered line."
- **Speaker note:** The cost-stack collapse and the single point of failure it rests on, in one beat.

## Slide 5
- **Kicker:** Pivot
- **Headline:** This isn't a story about a drug. It's a story about where margin goes when the molecule is free-ish.
- **Body:** When the input falls 85%, value migrates to what stays scarce: sterile fill-finish lines, the pen device, and the regulatory track record.
- **Hero stat:** ~3–4 isolator-based sterile fill-finish facilities exist in India — Zerodha Chatter, May 2026
- **Design note:** Single bold sentence on a clean background.
- **Speaker note:** The explicit thesis slide in the middle of the deck.

## Slide 6
- **Kicker:** The shovel sellers
- **Headline:** The cleanest economics belong to companies that launched nothing — and to one licensed pen.
- **Body:** OneSource closed its order book at 20+ customers on take-or-pay contracts; its clients hold ~two-thirds of the generic market by value, and capacity runs from ~40M to ~220M units by FY27. Gland signed 8 GLP-1 contracts at 140M cartridges a year. Zydus built the reusable titration pen and licensed it to Lupin and Torrent — 57% of April's generic injectable sales flowed through its lines. Torrent separately took 38% of generic share, including 100% of oral.
- **Hero stat:** 57% of generic injectables via Zydus's pen lines — Pharmatrac via Zerodha
- **Design note:** Three labelled boxes — fill-finish, device, intermediates — with capacity numbers inside, and one pen with three arrows converging on it.
- **Speaker note:** The dense evidence slide: where the durable money sits, and what decided the early share map.

## Slide 7
- **Kicker:** Reality check
- **Headline:** July brought recalls at three launchers. September brings a tariff clock.
- **Body:** Dr Reddy's, Torrent and USV voluntarily recalled select batches; Dr Reddy's supplies stay disrupted until late October or early November. On 29 September the US Section 232 regime fully phases in — up to 100% on patented pharmaceuticals and APIs, generics exempt but under formal review within a year. The tariff shields India's generic engine and bites the innovator-side CDMO work.
- **Hero stat:** 3 voluntary recalls (Jul 2026); 100% tariff fully effective 29 Sep 2026 — Medical Dialogues / EY
- **Design note:** Two muted panels — three dated recall notices on one side, a calendar callout on 29 September with "generics: exempt (under review)" on the other. No drama.
- **Speaker note:** The two repricing forces — quality risk and policy overlay — as one beat before the close.

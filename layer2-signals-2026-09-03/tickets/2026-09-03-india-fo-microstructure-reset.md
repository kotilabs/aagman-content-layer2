## Signal summary

Two years into SEBI's F&O reset, Indian options volumes are down >50% in FY26 and NSE's August equity-derivatives turnover hit ₹34.48 lakh crore — the lowest since November 2023. The unmapped second-order questions: what half the options flow was doing for cash-market liquidity and event-day price discovery, how the exchange duopoly's economics get rewritten (BSE briefly topped NSE in April on derivatives fees), and whether suppressed retail speculation flowed into mutual funds — or offshore into US futures (Tradeify) — i.e., exported rather than retired. Editorial wedge: Market Mechanics — what removing the world's largest retail options flow does to how Indian prices form.

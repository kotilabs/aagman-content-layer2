# Operator Ticket — India's Solar Curtailment Wall

**Signal ID:** india-solar-curtailment-wall  
**Publication date:** 2026-09-03  
**Selected from:** Macro Lens v2 — Primary Editorial Candidate #7

## Signal summary

India curtailed 8,133 GWh of solar generation in April–June 2026 — the first quarter where grid operators openly restricted generation at scale — right after a record 52.5 GW FY26 buildout. Transmission, not panels, is now the binding constraint. The financial loop: curtailment → depressed merchant power prices → weaker IPP cash flows → costlier capital for the next gigawatt — colliding with ICRA's discom-debt warnings and the draft National Electricity Policy 2026 reform push. Editorial wedge: the energy transition's constraint has moved from generation economics to grid plumbing, and the market hasn't repriced it.

the us joined japan's yen intervention on july 31, the first time washington has bought yen since 1998. japan spent ~$59bn solo on july 30, its largest single-day currency defense ever, and ~$75bn in total. the us leg was small on purpose: *$5-10bn*, per bessent's own notepad photographed at camp david.

the mechanism is the story. japan's reserves sit in us treasuries, so every yen defense begins with treasury selling, and the us 10-year was already at 4.6%. washington's operation was designed to keep those bonds off the market: the us sold *euros, not dollars*, and japan will now finance future interventions through the fed's fima repo facility, borrowing dollars against treasuries instead of selling them.

the us did not spend $5-10bn to move the yen. it spent $5-10bn to change how japan pays for moving it.

the us joined japan's yen intervention on july 31, the first time washington has bought yen since 1998.

the numbers:
- japan spent *~$59bn* solo on july 30, a record single day. ~$75bn in total
- the us leg was small on purpose: *$5-10bn*, per bessent's notepad photographed at camp david

why washington joined:
- japan's reserves sit in us treasuries, so every yen defense starts with treasury selling
- the us 10-year was already at *4.6%*. repeated solo defense meant a persistent new seller of us bonds
- so the us operation was built to keep treasuries off the market: it sold *euros, not dollars*
- and japan will now finance interventions through the fed's fima repo facility: borrow dollars against treasuries instead of selling them

the us did not spend $5-10bn to move the yen. it spent $5-10bn to change how japan pays for moving it.

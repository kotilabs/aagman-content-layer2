### Phase 3 — Recommended concepts

#### Concept 1: "The Sign Flip" — the petroleum footnote, visualized
- **Central insight:** The same Q1FY27 manufacturing dataset says "margins fell" and "margins grew 20.2%" — petroleum is the difference, and dispersion, not collapse, is the story.
- **Data:** Aggregate manufacturing operating profit down YoY; ex-petroleum +20.2% YoY (CMIE via Moneycontrol, 2026-08-15). Anchor beside: non-financial sales +22.5% (same source).
- **Visualization type:** Divergence / paired-bars. Two bars from one dataset — one below zero (unlabelled value, direction only, since CMIE gave no point value), one at +20.2%.
- **Composition notes:** Reader sees "Manufacturing profit, Q1FY27" title, then a red bar sinking left of a zero line; a second panel removes a small grey "petroleum" block and the bar swings to +20.2% green. Payoff line underneath: "Same companies. Same quarter. One sector's weight." Eye path: title → red bar → the removable block → green bar → payoff.
- **Sources:** CMIE data via Moneycontrol Pro Weekender, 2026-08-15 — https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/markets/moneycontrol-pro-weekender-at-79-strong-numbers-hard-choices-14005913.html
- **Required context label:** "Aggregate decline has no published point value in the source; direction only."

#### Concept 2: "The Revision Map" — upgrades vs downgrades by sector
- **Central insight:** Q1FY27's real outcome was a sorting: estimates rose where input costs and credit cycles helped, fell where demand and pricing were questioned.
- **Data:** Upgrades — basic materials/commodities, financials, industrials, consumer staples. Downgrades — consumer discretionary, health care, utilities (HSBC via Moneycontrol, 2026-08-07). Mechanism anchors: credit growth 18.3% June 2026 vs ~10% late 2025; PV sales −5–10% (mid-2025) → +15–25% (recent) (same source).
- **Visualization type:** Two-column divergence chart — green up-arrow tiles vs red down-arrow tiles, each annotated with its driver (credit 18.3% under financials; input costs under materials; demand question mark under discretionary).
- **Composition notes:** Reader sees the two columns first; the eye follows the annotations downward to a baseline strip: "73% beat or met — the count that hides this map." Payoff: the beats count and the revision map measure different things.
- **Sources:** HSBC Global Investment Research via Moneycontrol, 2026-08-07 — https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/markets/earnings-haven-t-cracked-the-way-the-market-feared-hsbc-data-shows-13997885.html
- **Required context label:** "Sector lists are directional from HSBC's note; no public net upgrade/downgrade count exists. IT shown separately — structural AI repricing, not cyclical cut (Infosys guidance narrowed to 1.5–3%, CNBC-TV18, 2026-07-23)."

#### Concept 3: "The Streak That Broke" — FPI flows meet earnings season
- **Central insight:** Foreign investors sold a record ~Rs 2.87 lakh crore into June, then turned buyer in July — just as the earnings data they will re-underwrite was landing.
- **Data:** Monthly FPI bars: Mar −Rs 1.18 lakh cr; Apr −Rs 60,847 cr; May −Rs 32,963 cr; Jun −Rs 49,340 cr; Jul +Rs 17,227 cr; Aug-to-date +Rs 12,921 cr (NSDL via BW Businessworld, 2026-07-30; ET Now, 2026-08-08). Overlay: Nifty −15.2% from January peak (Value Research, 2026-08-18). Season markers: TCS opens season Jul 9; HSBC note Aug 7; CMIE cut-off Aug 14.
- **Visualization type:** Timeline + flow — monthly diverging bar chart with event flags on the x-axis.
- **Composition notes:** Reader sees four deep red bars shrinking month by month, then two green bars. The eye lands on the July bar flagged "earnings season begins." Payoff line: "Returning capital underwrites revisions, not beats."
- **Sources:** NSDL data via https://www.businessworld.in/article/fpis-invest-rs-17-227-cr-in-indian-equities-in-july-end-four-month-selling-streak-616242 and https://www.etnownews.com/economy/fpi-buying-continues-foreign-investors-infuse-rs-12921-crore-into-indian-equities-in-august-so-far-bajaj-broking-cites-easing-geopolitical-tensions-article-155385819 ; Value Research — https://www.valueresearchonline.com/learn/stocks/nifty-50-pullback-5-criteria-stay-switch-exit/
- **Required context label:** "August figure is partial (through Aug 8). Flows are secondary-market equity only."

#### Concept 4: "The Pipeline Isn't the P&L" — IT's two-number problem
- **Central insight:** Indian IT's deal pipeline and its P&L have decoupled — outcome-based AI contracts defer and deflate recognised revenue even as bookings stay healthy.
- **Data:** TCS $9.5 bn Q1 deal wins with profit +5% YoY but −2.7% QoQ and brokerage-flagged flat dollar conversion (News18, 2026-07-09); Infosys FY27 CC guidance narrowed to 1.5–3% from 1.5–3.5% (CNBC-TV18, 2026-07-23); brokerage warnings of multi-year AI-deflation pressure (Moneycontrol Weekender, 2026-08-15; Fortune India, 2026-07-10).
- **Visualization type:** Flow/funnel — a wide "bookings" funnel compressing into a narrow "recognised revenue" pipe, with a valve labelled "outcome-based AI contracts."
- **Composition notes:** Reader sees the fat $9.5 bn funnel first; the eye follows the pipe narrowing rightward toward the guidance strip "1.5–3%". Payoff: "The pipeline measures intent. The P&L measures timing. AI moved the valve."
- **Sources:** https://www.news18.com/business/markets/tcs-q1-results-2026-live-updates-tata-consultancy-services-q1-earnings-share-price-today-revenue-net-profit-dividend-liveblog-ws-l-10199978.html ; https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/infosys-q1-results-live-updates-fy27-guidance-revenue-margin-deal-wins-adrs-pipeline-share-price-liveblog-19952124.htm ; https://www.fortuneindia.com/markets/tcs-leads-it-rally-jumps-4-infosys-hcltech-tech-mahindra-gain-as-street-backs-q1-earnings/147521
- **Required context label:** "No company has yet quantified the FY27 revenue-recognition impact; the mechanism is documented, the magnitude is not."

### What to avoid

- **A "beats vs misses" scoreboard (73% donut):** rejected — it visualizes exactly the framing this signal argues against, and the 73% number alone would misread as unqualified strength.
- **Korea/Taiwan growth comparison bars:** dropped — the comparators are unnamed estimates in a secondary source; a chart would imply precision the data doesn't carry.
- **"Nifty correction" standalone chart:** dropped — a drawdown line with no earnings data attached is generic market commentary, already well-visualized everywhere.
- **Company capex montage (HAL/PowerGrid/Kaynes numbers side by side):** dropped — order books use different definitions and horizons; a shared axis would fabricate comparability. The HAL-vs-MoSPI tension is retained as an annotated pair inside Concept 2's notes only if space allows.

### Final recommendation

Proceed with Concepts 1, 2 and 3. Concept 1 is the lead visual (simplest, most surprising, fully sourced); Concept 2 carries the editorial thesis (revision breadth); Concept 3 supplies the stakes. Concept 4 is strong but overlaps the carousel's IT slides — hold it as a follow-up piece if IT disclosures quantify the AI revenue-recognition impact later in FY27.

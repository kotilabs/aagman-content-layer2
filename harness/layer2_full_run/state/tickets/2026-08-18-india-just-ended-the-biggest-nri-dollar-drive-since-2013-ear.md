**Why this matters now**
On August 14, the RBI shortened the mobilisation window for its special FCNR(B) USD-INR swap facility to August 31 (from September 30), after forex inflows through the scheme crossed $56.8 billion — deposits mobilised by August 31 can be swapped with the RBI until September 11. The rupee has been Asia's worst-performing currency in 2026 (record lows near 96/USD in May), and the RBI is simultaneously running a defense operation and declaring it successful enough to end early.

**Primary asset classes affected**
USD/INR spot and forwards, onshore forward premia, Indian government bonds, bank balance sheets (NRI deposit franchises), FX reserves composition, EM FX broadly.

**What makes this structurally interesting**
This is financial plumbing as policy signal. The swap facility had the RBI absorbing the forward-hedging cost to make NRI dollar deposits attractive — effectively the central bank subsidizing reserve accumulation. Ending the window early, weeks after the Governor publicly ruled out early closure (August 5), tells you either the target was overshot or the carry cost of the swap book became uncomfortable. Either reading is editorially rich: the state of India's reserve adequacy and the forward book is being communicated through an operational notice, not a speech.

**What most commentary is missing**
Coverage is aimed at NRIs ("deposit before the deadline"). The market story is the *liability structure* the scheme created: tens of billions in tenor-matched swaps maturing in 3–5 years, the RBI sitting on the hedge leg, and the forward premia distortions that unwind as the window closes. Also missing: the RBI paired the scheme with a priority-sector-lending exclusion for loans against these deposits (August 7) — a quiet admission that NRI-dollar-funded credit growth needed ring-fencing.

**Known / Unknown / Unknowable**
- *Known:* window preponed to Aug 31 / swap until Sep 11; $56.8bn inflows cited; $36.7bn had been mobilised by July 31; rupee at record lows earlier this year; PSL exclusion notified August 7.
- *Unknown:* the RBI's internal target; the size and maturity distribution of the swap book; whether early closure reflects success or cost.
- *Unknowable:* how the forward book behaves if USD/INR retests record levels before the swaps season.

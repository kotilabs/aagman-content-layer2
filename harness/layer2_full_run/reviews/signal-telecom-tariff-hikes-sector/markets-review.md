## 5. Independent gap analysis (what all three outputs miss)

1. **The strongest counter to "rent extraction" is never engaged head-on.** Even after a 15% hike, Indian tariffs remain among the world's cheapest in absolute terms, and consumers extracted a decade of surplus (the blog's own close admits this: "the cheapest data in the world… funded by operator losses"). If the Jio-era price war was a transfer *to* consumers, the current ratchet is partly reversion, not pure extraction. The outputs gesture at this (Vittal's "lowest globally" line) but never let it land with numbers — e.g., ARPU ₹264 ≈ $3/month. The argument would be stronger for surviving it. (Optional)
2. **Spectrum installments undermine "funding returns, not construction."** The ₹1.5 lakh crore 2022 auction is being paid in deferred annual installments to the government; hike rupees service those bills for years. This is simultaneously a counter to the blog's B5 claim *and* further evidence for the fiscal reading (the state collects via spectrum too) — a richer point than either output makes. (Optional)
3. **Vi as price disciplinarian is asserted away, not analysed.** Vi's ₹190 ARPU versus Airtel's ₹264 is itself evidence that price differentiation persists at the entry tier — the "lockstep" narrative is about headline hikes, not realised prices. The artifact's own Open Question 5 ("at what point does the 3-player framing stop being descriptive?") is the sharpest version of this and no output picks it up. (Optional)
4. **Dual-SIM arbitrage is absent.** The artifact's unknowns include "dual-SIM to BSNL" as a churn response; a meaningful share of price-sensitive users can hold a BSNL secondary SIM rather than exit. This complicates "nowhere to churn to" — true for primary connectivity, less true for voice-only fallback. (Optional)
5. **BSNL's ₹100-class plans** (artifact, Interpretation D) — the actual escape-valve price point — are never named; "BSNL as anchor" stays abstract in all three outputs. (Optional)
6. **The postpaid/premium split of the hike's burden.** The hike is expected to be structurally progressive (heavier data users pay more); the regressive story is concentrated at the floor. The outputs correctly emphasise the floor but don't note the middle tiers may see smaller percentage rises — which matters for the inflation-transmission arithmetic. (Optional)
7. **Jefferies' timing slip** (June → Oct–Nov) is unremarked everywhere, though it is the single best evidence available about how this cycle actually works: the hike was pre-agreed in substance, flexible in calendar. (Should-fix for carousel C4; optional elsewhere.)

## 6. Verdict

**Blog: BLOCKED.** Two blockers in the same central paragraph (B1: unsourced ₹199 staircase step; B2: "40% in twelve months" contradicting the artifact's "40% in 24 months" and the blog's own +17% figure). The writer's flag on ₹199 was correct and it is a blocker, not a should-fix — the blog's hero mechanism currently rests on an invented number with wrong arithmetic. Fixes are surgical: delete the ₹199 step, correct the timeframe. Once done, this is a strong draft — the four-interpretations section, the analogue handling, and the mechanism explanation are at standard. Also resolve should-fixes B3–B5 before publication.

**Carousel: APPROVED WITH REQUIRED FIXES.** No blockers. Six should-fixes (C1–C6), all single-word or single-clause tense/attribution corrections; C7 requires one attribution line. The argument's compression is honest in structure and only sloppy in mood (indicative where it should be conditional). No-CTA rule compliant.

**Infographic: APPROVED WITH REQUIRED FIXES.** No blockers. Two should-fixes (I1, I2), both dating precision on the same staircase/trough facts. Claim-vs-data alignment is the best of the three surfaces; its avoid-list should be treated as binding on the other formats.

**Cross-surface:** The three surfaces currently tell three slightly different stories about the signal's most important number — the entry-floor staircase. Blog says ₹199→₹249→₹299→₹349, +40%/12mo (wrong); carousel and infographic say ₹249 (2024)→₹349, +40%/24mo (right, with an unsourced 2024 tag). Fix the blog to match the artifact, and strip or source the "(2024)" tag everywhere.

**Recommended disposition:** Return to writers for one revision pass: blog B1–B5 mandatory; carousel C1–C7; infographic I1–I2. Gaps in §5 are optional enrichments; none blocks publication.

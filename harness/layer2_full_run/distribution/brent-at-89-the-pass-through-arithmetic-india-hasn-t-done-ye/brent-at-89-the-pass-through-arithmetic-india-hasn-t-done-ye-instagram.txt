$89 oil is costing india what $112 oil cost in 2022. the rupee did the rest.

brent crude settled at $89 a barrel on august 17. the rupee closed at 95.61 per dollar, about 1% off its record low. multiply the two and one barrel costs india ₹8,509, roughly ₹53.5 a litre of crude before it gets refined, taxed and pumped.

in may 2022, brent at $112 with the rupee at 77.8 cost ₹8,728 a barrel. barely 3% more than today. in january this year the same barrel cost about ₹5,670. the rupee cost of crude is up 50% in seven months, and about a sixth of that climb came from the currency sliding, the rest from the oil itself.

your pump price has not moved. delhi petrol has sat at ₹102.12 since may 15, when a ₹3 hike broke a four-year freeze. the squeeze lands on oil company margins instead: breakeven sits around brent $92-99 at this exchange rate, so at $89 the cushion is already thin. when crude was above $100 in april, state oil companies were bleeding close to ₹30,000 crore a month.

one more thing worth saving: the may hike showed up in june's inflation print within a month (3.93% to 4.38%). the august print lands september 14.

india is paying about ₹65 crore a month to rent two drones it already bought.

on august 17, the defence ministry signed a ₹1,943 crore, 30-month lease for two mq-9b sea guardian drones. the navy already flies two leased ones, and has been waiting since october 2024 on a $3.99 billion order for 31. the lease is the interest india pays on slow delivery.

one august week, four big-ticket india stories: a drone lease, a mega-factory proposal, a homegrown chip, a launch-cost record. financing is the story.

the numbers:

- *₹1,943 crore* to lease two mq-9b drones for 30 months, signed 17 august (pib). roughly *₹65 crore a month* for the pair.
- the navy already leases two mq-9bs and waits on a *$3.99 billion* order for 31, signed october 2024.
- adani proposed an *₹80,000 crore* coal-to-chemicals plant, two 3,000 mw nuclear units, and an aluminium complex in odisha. proposals only; nothing sanctioned yet.
- the group has raised or approved over *₹50,000 crore* of equity in 18 months.
- aheesa taped out vihaan-i, a 28nm risc-v chip, in february under meity's dli scheme.
- launch cost estimate: *$13,302 per kg*, highest among major spacefaring nations; the us is at $3,225. five launches in 2025.

why it hangs together: four ways to pay for strategy: conglomerate equity, defence leasing, design subsidies, and space with no instrument yet.

takeaway: announcement volume is not capex. the paper trail is.

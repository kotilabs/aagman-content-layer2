the rbi closed its nri dollar window a month early, and the interesting part is that there was no announcement in the speech sense. nine days after the governor said there were no plans to close it, an operational notice did.

three facts worth holding together. the scheme pulled $52.3 billion of fcnr(b) deposits in ten weeks, double the 2013 haul, with the last $16 billion arriving in 13 days. the rbi absorbed the entire hedging cost at par, which is what made 7.8% dollar deposit rates possible. and over the scheme's whole life, the rupee moved from 95.39 to 95.45.

the underreported detail is the august 7 companion notice: loans against these fresh deposits were excluded from priority-sector calculations. credit funded by nri dollars was ring-fenced from the regulated lending baseline a week before the tap was shut.

so the early close reads as oversubscription success, or as a subsidy whose carry became uncomfortable on a forward book already reportedly $106.6 billion short at end-may. both fit. what neither answers: where usd/inr stands when the swaps come due in 2029-2031. the fuller treatment is on the aagman substack this week.

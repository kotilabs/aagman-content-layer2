the rbi closed its biggest nri dollar drive since 2013 *a month early*, nine days after the governor said there were _no plans to_.

why the scheme existed: the rupee hit a record low of *96.34* in may and spent the year as _asia's worst-performing currency_. india needed dollars, fast.

- the scheme let banks swap nri dollars with the rbi, which ate the hedging cost, so deposits paid up to *7.8%*
- *$56.85 billion* came in: the first $20bn took 38 days, the last $16bn took 13. that is _double the 2013 crisis haul, in a third of the time_
- the rupee still went 95.39 to *95.45*. _reserves were bought, relief was not_
- those dollars must be returned in 2029-2031 at today's rate

takeaway: either the rbi got its dollars early, or the hedge was getting expensive. the governor _ruled that out nine days earlier_.

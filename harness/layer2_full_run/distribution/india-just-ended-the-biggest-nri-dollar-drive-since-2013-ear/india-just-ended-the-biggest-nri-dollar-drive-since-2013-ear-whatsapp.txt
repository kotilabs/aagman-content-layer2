the rbi closed its biggest nri dollar drive since 2013 a month early, nine days after the governor said there were no plans to.

- the scheme let banks swap nri dollars with the rbi, which ate the hedging cost, so deposits paid up to *7.8%*
- *$56.85 billion* came in: the first $20bn took 38 days, the last $16bn took 13. that is double the 2013 crisis haul, in a third of the time
- the rupee still went 95.39 to *95.45*. reserves were bought, relief was not
- those dollars must be returned in 2029-2031 at today's rate

takeaway: either the rbi got its dollars early, or the hedge was getting expensive. the governor ruled that out nine days earlier.

the full piece is on the aagman substack this week: how it worked, the quiet rule change a week before the closure, and whose money it really was.

the rbi closed its biggest nri dollar drive since 2013 *a month early*. nine days earlier, the governor had said _no plans to_.

why: the rupee hit a record *96.34* in may, _asia's worst-performing currency_ this year. india needed dollars.

- banks swapped nri dollars with the rbi, which ate the hedging cost, so deposits paid up to *7.8%*
- *$56.85 billion* came in: the first $20bn took 38 days, the last $16bn took 13. _double the 2013 crisis haul in a third of the time_
- the rupee still went 95.39 to *95.45*. _reserves were bought, relief was not_
- those dollars must be returned in 2029-2031 at today's rate

takeaway: either the rbi got its dollars early, or the hedge got expensive. the governor _ruled that out nine days earlier_.

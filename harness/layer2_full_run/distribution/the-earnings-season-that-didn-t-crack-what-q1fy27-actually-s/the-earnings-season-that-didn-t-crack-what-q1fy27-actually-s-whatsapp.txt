q1fy27 earnings season is over. the aggregate held, the interior split.

the numbers:

- non-financial net sales grew *22.5%* yoy, per cmie data
- *73%* of results in line or better, top of hsbc's six-year band
- manufacturing operating profit fell yoy; excluding petroleum it grew *20.2%*
- non-food credit growth hit *18.3%* in june, from roughly 10% in late 2025
- infosys narrowed fy27 guidance to *1.5-3%*; tcs won *$9.5bn* in deals while revenue barely moved

why the headline misleads:

- a beats count measures results against expectations set before the season
- revision breadth measures what analysts did to full-year numbers after
- upgrades: basic materials, financials, industrials, staples
- downgrades: consumer discretionary, health care, utilities
- nobody published the net upgrade-minus-downgrade count

the stakes: the nifty sits *15.2%* below its january peak. fpis sold a record *rs 2.87 lakh crore* by mid-june, then bought *rs 17,227 crore* in july. returning capital underwrites revisions.

the full piece on the aagman substack this week gets into what doesn't fit here: the petroleum footnote, ai revenue recognition in it, and what september's estimate revisions settle.

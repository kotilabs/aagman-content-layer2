india's q1fy27 season closed with a clean headline: non-financial net sales up 22.5% year on year, 73% of results in line or better per hsbc, near the top of a six-year band. there was no crack, and technically there was also no verdict.

the beats count measures results against expectations as they stood in june. revision breadth measures what analysts did to full-year numbers afterwards. since june-end, upgrades clustered in basic materials, financials, industrials, staples; downgrades in consumer discretionary, health care, utilities. the net count, upgrades minus downgrades across the market, has not been published anywhere. the consensus still assumes roughly 14% growth for 2026 and 17% for 2027.

the underreported detail is petroleum. aggregate manufacturing operating profit fell year on year; excluding petroleum products it grew 20.2%. one sector's weight flipped the sign of the whole profit pool, and both readings come from the same table.

so the open question is mechanical: do the september estimate revisions stabilise, or does the fy27 number keep drifting while the season is remembered as a beat. the returning fpi money, 17,227 crore rupees in july after a record 2.87 lakh crore walked out, will underwrite whichever answer arrives first.

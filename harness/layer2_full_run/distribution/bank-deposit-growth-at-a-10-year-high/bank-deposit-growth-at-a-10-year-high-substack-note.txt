india's deposit growth hit 15.4% yoy as of july 31, the fastest in over a decade, and the interesting part is that nobody's savings behaviour changed. growth was 12.7% on july 15. a 270-bps move in one fortnight, on a deposit base above rs 260 trillion, is not something households can do. it is something a swap window can do.

hold three facts together. deposits fell rs 3.87 trillion between april 1 and june 15. the rbi opened a special fcnr(b) dollar-swap facility on june 8, and it drew $52.3 billion before being closed early. and $36.7 billion of that converted into rupees in the same fortnights the print jumped.

the underreported detail: the rbi changed its fortnight reporting convention in december 2025, and the july data is provisional. the decade-high label survives that caveat, but the composition question remains, because the rbi does not publish how much of the 15.4% is conversion versus organic growth.

the window takes its last deposits on august 31. the mid-august print, out early september, is where we find out what is left.

## Hypothesis

On a sub-100-follower LinkedIn company page, India-market-focused single-image infographics will reach significantly more impressions than multi-page carousels covering the same topic, because single-image posts are more likely to be distributed by the algorithm.

Evidence so far:
- Rupee–Equity divergence (single-image infographic): 1,208 impressions
- China two-speeds (5-page carousel): 115 impressions
- Gold export (chalkboard comic carousel): 46 impressions

---

## Variants

### Variant A (control): multi-page carousel
- 4–6 slide carousel with chart + explanatory text
- Long intro copy above the carousel

### Variant B (test): single-image infographic
- One clean chart/data visualization
- ≤2 line intro copy

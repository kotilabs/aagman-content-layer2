# X (Twitter) formatter

Turn the signal's source material into an X post.

- register: feed punch. lowercase, direct address ("you"), written to stop the
  scroll
- lead with the mechanism or the number, never a summary of someone else's post
- ~150-200 words for long-form (Premium accounts; posted via the browser flow in
  x-finance-scout/WORKFLOW.md). if the account is free-tier, cut to ≤ 280 chars:
  hook + one number + the implication
- structure: what happened / the pattern with dates / why they get away with it /
  what it means for you / the one-line takeaway (a fact, not a slogan)
- media: up to 4 images. pick the slides that carry the story arc
  (hook / structure / evidence / who pays); the text must cover whatever the
  dropped slides said
- no hashtags in the body; at most 1-2 cashtags if genuinely relevant

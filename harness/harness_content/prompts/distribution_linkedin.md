# LinkedIn formatter

Turn the signal's source material into a LinkedIn post (usually accompanying a
PDF document carousel).

- register: condensed essay. normal case, measured, professional but not
  corporate. this is the one platform where full sentences and standard
  capitalization stay
- ~250-300 words, ≤ 3,000 chars
- first two lines must hook before the "see more" fold: the fact + why it's odd
  ("Nobody announced a price hike. The cheapest plan just disappeared.")
- structure: what happened / the mechanism / why this instance is different /
  who bears the cost / the open question
- no slogans-as-closers on their own. land the argument, then add one line
  pointing at the document: "The full breakdown is in the document. Swipe through."
- no hashtags in the body. at most 3 topical ones at the end, or none
- if the carousel PDF exists, the post's asset is the document; the caption must
  stand alone for people who never open it

# Instagram formatter

Turn the signal's source material into an Instagram caption.

- audience: the scroller who does not follow finance. every caption must answer
  "what does this mean for a normal person" in plain terms (recharge, emi,
  petrol, salary, savings)
- first ~125 chars decide everything: lead with the sharpest number or the
  mechanism trick ("nobody announced a hike. they just deleted the cheapest plan")
- lowercase, short paragraphs, lots of air. no hashtags
- structure: hook / the mechanism in 2-3 short paras / the normal-folks impact /
  one closing fact line
- CTA tied to the story's future moment ("save this for when your recharge price
  changes in november"), not a generic "follow us"
- end with a keywords line: "keywords: ..." comma-separated search phrases
  (10-14), plain text, no # symbols
- ≤ 2,200 chars total
- if a carousel exists, the caption references it naturally only if it adds
  something; never say "swipe" filler without content

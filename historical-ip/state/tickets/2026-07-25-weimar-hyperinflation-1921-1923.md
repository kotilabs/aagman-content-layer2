## Selected story

- **Story ID:** weimar-hyperinflation-1921-1923
- **Title:** From Four Marks to Four Trillion: The Weimar Hyperinflation, 1921–1923
- **Hook:** In five years the German mark went from 4 to the dollar to 4.2 trillion to the dollar — and an entire society learned what money is when it stops working.
- **Period / asset:** 1921–1923, Weimar Germany / paper mark currency.
- **Why it matters now:** It is the clearest recorded case of what happens when a government funds itself by printing money: prices, exchange rates, and social trust collapse together.
- **Educational spine:**
  1. Hyperinflation is a fiscal phenomenon dressed up as a monetary one.
  2. A currency's value rests on collective belief; once that belief breaks, velocity accelerates the damage.
  3. Stabilization only works when fiscal policy is credibly anchored to a new unit and a new set of rules.
- **Core characters / forces:** The Reichsbank, Rudolf Havenstein, Hugo Stinnes, Gustav Stresemann, Hjalmar Schacht, the Rentenmark.

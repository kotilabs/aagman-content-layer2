three fed presidents voted to hike rates in july. hammack now says it may take "some number" of hikes to bring inflation down. futures markets still price the next move as a cut.

the us government already pays over $1 trillion a year in interest alone, more than the entire defense budget. every 25bp hike adds tens of billions to that bill. the bigger the bill, the more the government borrows. the more it borrows, the higher rates stay.

so the fed wants to slow prices down with medicine that makes the government's debt problem worse.

inflation is 4.2%. your savings lose value every month they sit in a deposit. if the hawks win, your emis and card rates climb on top of that. at the store or on your loans, the bill lands with you.

three presidents looked at this loop and voted to hike anyway. watch your emi if more of them join.

india's july trade deficit came in at $32 billion. june last year was $19 billion.

and it isn't one bad month. january was $34.7b, april $28.4b, may $28.2b, june $30.4b, now july $32b. the deficit didn't spike. it moved to a new address.

oil is half the story. gold is the other half. indians imported $84 billion of gold and silver last year, up 34%, at the highest prices ever recorded. households, not factories, are the marginal buyer.

your neighbour is converting savings into gold because they don't trust where prices are going. multiply that by a hundred million families and it lands in customs data.

you feel the other half at the pump, and in any store selling anything imported. the rupee sits at 94-95 a dollar, and rbi just raised its inflation forecast to 5.1%.

exports are at record highs. the selling side is fine. what changed is fear, and july's number is what fear looks like when a whole country has it.

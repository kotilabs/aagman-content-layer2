## Angle

Fiscal dominance made concrete: a Fed minority wants hikes to fight 4.2% inflation, but every 25bp adds tens of billions to a $1T+ interest bill → wider deficit → more issuance → upward yield pressure. The brake pedal is connected to the gas tank. Consumer pays twice: 4.2% at the store, plus higher loan rates if hawks win.

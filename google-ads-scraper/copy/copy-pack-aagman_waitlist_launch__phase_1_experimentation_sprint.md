# Copy Pack: Aagman Waitlist Launch — Phase 1 Experimentation Sprint

**Objective:** Drive 500 verified (double opt-in confirmed) waitlist signups in 6 weeks while identifying the cheapest-converting segment and messaging angle to inform Q2 scaling.
**Audience:** Three test segments from source-of-truth personas: S1 — active NIFTY/BANKNIFTY F&O traders on Zerodha/Dhan frustrated by limited order types (45% of test budget); S2 — rule-based retail traders without coding skills (35%); S3 — long-term quality-stock investors and screener users (20%). Excluded: MF-only buyers, multi-language-first users (Phase 2), crypto/forex/international audiences.
**Success Metric:** 500 verified waitlist signups at blended CPA <= Rs.150; pause any ad group with CPA > Rs.250 after ~Rs.3,000 spend; minimum Rs.5,000 spend per test cell before judging; track signup-to-activation as secondary quality signal.

**Audience:** Active Indian retail F&O and equity traders (implied by the Draconic/Tradomate competitor set — ICP is unconfirmed in the brief; if the primary audience is long-term investors, variant prioritization must be redone and the investor angle promoted to lead)
**Success Metric:** Waitlist signup volume vs. current baseline — AMBIGUOUS: no numeric target, CPL ceiling, or timeframe is defined, and no baseline waitlist number exists to measure the 'spike' against. Recommend setting a signup target and max cost-per-signup before launch.

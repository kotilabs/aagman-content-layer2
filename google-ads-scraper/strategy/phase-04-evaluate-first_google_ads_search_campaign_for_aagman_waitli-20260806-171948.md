## 2. Top 5 Angles

**1. "Backtest a strategy in one sentence"** (Angle 3, total 18)
- **Why it won:** The most concrete, demonstrable version of the product promise — plain-language input, full institutional-grade report out. Directly executable against the likely "backtest trading strategy / free backtest" demand cluster (provisional). Draconic sells opinions; tradomate sells screens — neither owns "describe it in one line, get proof back."
- **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §6.1 (natural-language prompt grammar + example prompts), §8.2 (backtest report outputs); §16: natural-language strategy creation and backtesting both "Live — Yes."

**2. "Prove it before you trade it"** (Angle 1, total 18)
- **Why it won:** The differentiated philosophy angle — Backtest → Paper → Live is a workflow claim no competitor makes (draconic explicitly disclaims execution; tradomate stops at screening). Serves as the umbrella message for the trader-facing campaign, with Angle 3 as its tactical proof. Caution: the two angles must map to separate ad groups/intents so they don't cannibalize each other.
- **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §4.1 (enforced Idea → Backtest → Paper → Live workflow), §8.1 (look-ahead-safe mechanics), §14 pillar 2. Note: paper-trading step phrased as workflow only (§16 "Partially").

**3. "TWAP, VWAP, trailing stops — order types your broker app never shows you"** (Angle 6, total 17)
- **Why it won:** Highest differentiation in the set (5/5) — zero competitor coverage of execution depth; draconic is analysis-only, tradomate is screening-only. Fully safe: TWAP/VWAP are live per §16, and the retail-broker comparison table in §7.2 is already source-verified. Executability is the watch-out: "TWAP/VWAP" is vendor-speak as keywords — the searchable form is broker-anchored ("algo trading zerodha" pattern), which *must* be validated in Keyword Planner before this angle gets budget.
- **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §7.1 (order types & algos — TWAP/VWAP live; **exclude** Iceberg/POV, marked coming-next), §7.2 (retail broker comparison), §14 pillar 4.

**4. "No code, no Excel, no Python — describe the rule, deploy the strategy"** (Angle 13, total 16)
- **Why it won:** Targets the no-code systematic user persona and the broad "algo trading" demand cluster without requiring programming-intent searchers. Positive framing (states what the product IS) — compliant with the playbook's no-negative-only-headlines rule. Both competitors leave this persona unserved: draconic's jargon-dense copy targets sophisticated traders; tradomate targets screeners.
- **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §2 (no-code systematic users), §6.1 (prompt grammar replaces scripting), §14 pillar 7.

**5. "A stock screener that doesn't stop at screening"** (Angle 4, total 16)
- **Why it won:** Meets tradomate's most contested territory ("AI Stock Screener India") but with a structural differentiator they can't copy: screen → backtest the screen itself. Wins on message match for the high-probability "stock screener / free stock screener india" cluster while pre-empting the "just another screener" objection in one line.
- **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §3 (Screener agent), §6.2 (screener metrics), §4.4 investor workflow step 2 (screen → backtest); §16: screener and backtesting both "Live — Yes."

**First alternate:** Angle 11 ("Compare mutual funds like an analyst," 16) — swap it in for Angle 4 or 13 if the ICP is confirmed to include the MF-buyer persona, or if demand data shows the MF cluster ("best mutual funds," "mutual fund comparison") materially out-sizing the screener cluster.

## Provisional flag
These selections rank *angles*, not keywords. Before budget shares or ad-group structure are finalized, the seed list behind each selected angle must go through Keyword Planner — particularly the broker-anchored variants for Angle 6 and the "free"-modified variants for Angles 3 and 4. Any angle whose cluster comes back under ~50 monthly searches gets rerouted to descriptions/sitelinks/LP rather than killed outright.

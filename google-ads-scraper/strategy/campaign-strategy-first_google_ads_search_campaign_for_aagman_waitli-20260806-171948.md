## 1. Crowded Angles (both competitors are already here)

### A. "AI second opinion / ask before you trade"
- Draconic's *dominant* positioning: "AI Second Opinion for F&O," "Ask AI Before Every Trade," "F&O Setup? Ask First."
- This is also **Āagman content pillar #1** ("Ask before you trade"). Entering it head-on means competing on Draconic's home turf with a 5-month head start in creative iteration.
- **Implication:** Do not abandon it (it's our most natural trader angle), but don't lead with it as the differentiator. Win it on message match and LP quality, not novelty.

### B. "Free, no card, no friction"
- Draconic: "Free signup, no card" / "No KYC. 2 Minutes" in ~70% of ads. Tradomate: "Sign up free," "Free during beta," "Free pre-built screens."
- **Implication:** "Free in beta, no credit card" is now table stakes, not a hook. It belongs in descriptions and as message match for "free X" keywords — it will not differentiate. The one variant only Draconic tested ("3 Free Analyses Daily" — a *concrete usage cap*) suggests specificity beats generic "free," and nobody is repeating it.

### C. AI stock screener for NSE / Indian equities
- Tradomate is heavily concentrated here: "AI Stock Screener India," "Low PE Stock Screener - Value Stocks Screener NSE," "PE, ROE, Debt for NSE," breakout/momentum screens, chart patterns, price alerts.
- This is keyword-shaped copy ("AI Stock Screener India - Breakouts in Seconds") — clearly built for search-intent harvesting.
- **Implication:** Screener intent is contested but real. Āagman can enter (screener is safe to claim, including fundamental metrics like ROE/ROCE/debt), but generic "AI stock screener" copy will blend into Tradomate's wall of identical ads.

### D. "Replace your tool stack / one screen"
- Draconic: "Stop switching between TradingView, Sensibull, and 3 news tabs," "Replace 3 tools."
- **Implication:** Consolidation is claimed but shallowly — neither competitor demonstrates *what* replaces the stack. Ownable only with a concrete mechanism (see gaps).

### E. "AI picks stocks for you" / signals-adjacent
- Tradomate: "Let AI Pick Your Stocks - Free AI Stock Finder," "trade setups, breakouts, signals." Draconic flirts with "AI That Trades for You" while simultaneously disclaiming "Not a trading bot."
- **Implication:** This is the most crowded *and* most dangerous territory (see Section 3).

## 2. Ownable Gaps (credibly differentiated, safe to claim)

### G1. Backtest → Paper → Live: proof before capital
- **Competitor evidence:** Neither competitor mentions backtesting, paper trading, or staged deployment in any captured ad. Draconic sells *opinion*; Tradomate sells *screens*. Nobody sells *evidence*.
- **Āagman truth:** Fully safe to claim — backtesting (with look-ahead-safe fill logic), the enforced Backtest → Paper → Live workflow, kill switches, circuit breakers. Pillar #2.
- **Why it's ownable:** It's a workflow, not a feature. Copying it credibly requires rebuilding the product, not rewriting an ad.
- **Demand caveat:** "backtest trading strategy" / "backtest software india" likely have search volume; "paper trading app india" probably does too. "Proof-first trading" as a phrase has zero volume — it's a headline/LP concept, not a keyword. Verify via Keyword Planner before committing ad groups.

### G2. Execution depth: order types and algos retail brokers don't expose
- **Competitor evidence:** Zero mentions of TWAP, VWAP, trailing stops, MIT/LIT, or multi-leg execution from either competitor. Both are analysis-side products.
- **Āagman truth:** TWAP/VWAP live, 9 execution-native order types, 4-leg options strategies — all safe to claim (pillar #4).
- **Why it's ownable:** Draconic actively disclaims execution ("Not a trading bot"); Tradomate's entire inventory is screeners and alerts. This is structurally uncontested.
- **Demand caveat:** Playbook warning applies hard here — nobody searches "MIT/LIT/MTL" or "multi-leg execution." Searchable forms are likely "algo trading zerodha" (broker-anchored, a real India pattern), "twap vwap algo", "options strategy builder." This angle probably has **thin but high-intent** volume. Treat as a small exact-match cluster, not a budget pillar, until demand data confirms.

### G3. Compliance as a first-class claim: SEBI-registered IA + NSE-empanelled Whitebox algo provider
- **Competitor evidence:** Neither competitor shows any regulatory credential in ads. Draconic's constant "Not a trading bot, no signals, no tips" is a *defensive* compliance posture — a shield, not a credential.
- **Āagman truth:** SEBI IA (INA000021951), NSE Whitebox empanelment, auditable algo IDs, DPDP-aligned, India-hosted data. Pillar #5. Safe to claim.
- **Why it's ownable:** Registration numbers can't be copied by a competitor who doesn't have them.
- **Placement rule:** Per playbook, trust fragments are **not headlines**. Use in descriptions, sitelinks ("SEBI-Registered Investment Adviser"), and LP. On the LP it directly answers the trust objection Draconic spends 60% of its copy on.

### G4. "You approve every order — capital never leaves your broker"
- **Competitor evidence:** Draconic's "you make the call" is the closest, but it's framed as a disclaimer. Nobody frames *approval + capital custody* as the product's core architecture.
- **Āagman truth:** "You brief, they work, you approve." Not a broker; orders route through the user's existing broker. Pillars #7/#8. Safe to claim, including multi-broker (documented for Zerodha; adapters for Dhan, Groww, Angel One, MOFSL).
- **Why it's ownable:** It reframes the bot-vs-not-bot debate the whole category is stuck in. Positive framing ("approve every order") beats negative framing ("not a bot") — and the playbook explicitly endorses positive-over-negative.
- **Demand caveat:** This is an **education play, not a search play**. No one searches "capital stays at my broker." Route to ad descriptions, LP hero copy, and later remarketing — not keywords.

### G5. Multi-language / vernacular investing
- **Competitor evidence:** None. All captured creative is English-only, jargon-dense (OI, PCR, Greeks).
- **Āagman truth:** Prompts in Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu — safe to claim (pillar #6). Public example prompts exist in Hinglish and Hindi.
- **Why it's ownable:** Language support is a product property, not a copy choice.
- **Demand caveat:** Hindi-language search queries for trading tools exist but attribution/QA is harder; also risk of low-intent volume. Keep as a *later test* — a small phrase-match cluster in Hindi ("शेयर बाजार स्क्रीनर"-type terms) only after English clusters prove out. For now, "invest in your language" is an LP/sitelink differentiator.

### G6. Mutual fund + investor-side workflows
- **Competitor evidence:** Both competitors are 100% trader/F&O-focused. Zero MF, ELSS, expense-ratio, or long-term-investor creative.
- **Āagman truth:** MF screening by returns/expense ratio/category/holdings, quality-compounder screens (ROE/ROCE/debt) — all safe to claim today. (Portfolio health score, tax insights, news feed are **not** safe — coming soon.)
- **Demand caveat:** "best elss funds," "low expense ratio mutual funds," "mutual fund screener" are known high-volume India queries — but they skew informational/SEO. This could be a cheap-CPC second campaign if the ICP includes investors. **The extracted brief doesn't specify ICP** — if the waitlist campaign is trader-only, hold this for campaign #2.

## 4. Differentiation Recommendation

**Position against the category split, not against either competitor.** The market has sorted itself into two camps:

- **Draconic = opinion** (ask AI, then you figure out execution)
- **Tradomate = screens** (find stocks, then you figure out everything else)

**Āagman's ownable territory is the third thing: evidence + execution.** Neither competitor claims any of: backtesting, paper trading, staged deployment, risk gates, execution algos, or regulatory credentials. That's the white space, and every element of it is safe to claim.

**Recommended angle stack for the ₹75K Search test (priority order):**

1. **"Prove it before you trade it"** — backtest/paper angle. Lead angle: uncontested, product-true, and naturally pre-qualifies serious users (better for *verified* signup CPA than curiosity clicks). Seed research: backtest / paper trading / strategy testing terms.
2. **"Screener, but it goes further"** — compete in the contested screener intent pool (it's where the volume is), but differentiate in descriptions: screen → backtest → deploy, one chat. Message-match headlines stay keyword-shaped; differentiation lives below the headline.
3. **"Ask before you trade" (second opinion)** — enter, but as a challenger with a concrete twist (the second opinion comes with a backtest attached). Do not outshout Draconic on generic "AI second opinion."
4. **Execution/algos cluster** — small exact-match test against broker-anchored terms ("algo trading zerodha" pattern). High intent, thin volume. Scale only if Keyword Planner shows demand.
5. **Trust layer (not an angle — a layer):** SEBI IA + NSE empanelment in descriptions and sitelinks across all ad groups, plus LP. This neutralizes the #1 objection Draconic spends its entire budget defending against.

**Explicitly NOT in this Search campaign (routed elsewhere):**
- Capital-custody/approval framing → LP hero + remarketing copy (education play, no search demand).
- Multi-language → post-test expansion, small Hindi cluster.
- MF/investor workflows → hold pending ICP confirmation in the brief; likely a separate cheap-CPC campaign.
- Portfolio health / tax / news feed / MCX / Iceberg-POV → unshipped; excluded from all copy per source of truth.

**One unresolved dependency:** "cheapest verified signup per angle" requires the offline-conversion plumbing (GCLID capture + verified-lead upload) from the missing-items list. Without it, angle comparison will optimize toward form-fillers, and this gap analysis won't translate into real learnings. Confirm tracking readiness before launch; everything above is otherwise ready to turn into seed-keyword research requests.

## 2. Competitive Gap Analysis

# Competitive Gap Analysis — Āagman vs. Draconic & Tradomate

## Crowded angles

Angles both competitors (and the category generally) are already spending heavily on. Entering these head-on means paying auction premiums for undifferentiated positioning:

- **"AI second opinion / ask before you trade"** — Draconic's dominant positioning across ~90% of its inventory. Note: this is also content pillar #1 in our source of truth, so we *can* use it, but we cannot lead with it as our differentiator. It's theirs now.
- **Screener-led SEO harvesting** — Tradomate's entire program: "breakout stocks," "low PE screener," "gainers today," "chart patterns NSE," "dividend yield." Generic keyword-capture with thin product claims. High volume, low moat.
- **Institutional democratization** — "Think like an FII desk," "what prop desks see in seconds." Emotional retail-vs-institution gap. Draconic owns the copy territory, even if the underlying claim is vague.
- **Friction removal as the offer** — "Free signup, no card," "No KYC, 2 minutes," "3 free analyses daily" in ~70% of Draconic ads. "Free" alone is table stakes in this category, not a hook.
- **"AI picks stocks for you" / discovery feeds** — Tradomate's "Let AI Pick Your Stocks," "Discover Bullish Stocks," "tells you what to watch next." Sails close to tip/signal territory.
- **Anti-bot disclaimers** — "Not a trading bot, no signals, no tips" repeated defensively by Draconic. The category is defined by what it *isn't*.
- **Conquesting callouts** — Draconic directly names Zerodha, Dhan, Sahi, TradingView, Sensibull. Aggressive, and a brand/compliance risk we'd need an explicit guardrail decision on (flagged as ambiguous in the brief).

## Ownable gaps

Gaps where Āagman has **verified, source-of-truth-backed capabilities** that neither competitor is claiming — or credibly *can* claim:

1. **Proof-first execution workflow: Backtest → Paper → Live.**
   Neither competitor mentions backtesting, paper trading, or promotion gates. Tradomate stops at screening; Draconic stops at analysis. Āagman is the only one that takes an idea to *evidence* and then to *deployment*. This is our structural moat and maps to content pillar #2.

2. **Institutional order types and execution algos — actually shipped.**
   MIT, LIT, MTL, trailing stops, multi-leg options up to 4 legs in sync, **TWAP/VWAP live today**. Draconic gestures at "institutional" as a metaphor; Āagman has the literal execution stack. Competitors have zero copy in this space. (Careful: Iceberg/POV are "coming next" — not claimable.)

3. **Approval-gated execution as a positive frame.**
   Draconic's contradiction ("Not a bot" vs. "AI that trades for you") reveals the category's trust anxiety. Āagman resolves it cleanly: *"You brief, they work, you approve."* Positive control framing instead of defensive disclaimers — and it's architecturally true (not a bot that deploys without approval; capital stays at your broker).

4. **Regulatory credentials nobody else is running on.**
   SEBI-registered Investment Adviser (INA000021951) + NSE-empanelled Whitebox algo provider. Neither competitor surfaces any regulatory status. In a category full of "trust us" disclaimers, verifiable registration is a hard-to-copy trust asset — especially in Search, where intent is high and scrutiny is higher.

5. **Trade in the language you think in.**
   Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu prompting. Both competitors are English-only across 100% of observed creative. Draconic's late "Built for Indian Traders" pivot shows localization resonance, but they mean it as geography; we can mean it as *language*. Untapped in ad inventory.

6. **Investor / mutual-fund workflows.**
   Tradomate touches "low PE" screens, but no competitor addresses MF screening (expense ratio, ELSS, holdings overlap) or the long-term investor at all. The entire category is fighting over the F&O trader. (Guardrail: portfolio health score, tax insights, holdings news are "coming soon" — we can sell screening + backtesting to investors today, not the dashboard.)

7. **Pricing counter-position: "legacy rates forever" vs. ₹249/month.**
   Tradomate is actively pushing a paid subscription upgrade. "Free while in beta + early users lock legacy rates forever" is a concrete urgency hook that undercuts them — and it's more specific than the generic "free signup" flooding the category.

## Claims competitors make that we should NOT mirror

- **"Let AI pick your stocks" / signal-style discovery** (Tradomate) — conflicts with Āagman's "not a tip/signal service" identity and creates SEBI IA optics risk. Our frame is *user-initiated* screening, not AI-pushed picks.
- **"AI that trades for you" / "it executes"** (Draconic's contradictory variants) — we must never imply autonomous deployment. Every execution claim must carry the approval gate.
- **"See what FIIs positioned today" / prop-desk data claims** — unverified data capability; we have no source-of-truth support for institutional flow data.
- **Fear-stat urgency ("91% of F&O traders lose")** — even though a SEBI study underpins the number, leading with loss-fear in a first campaign invites ad-policy friction and borrows their exact emotional playbook.
- **Direct competitor name callouts** — the brief leaves conquesting ambiguous. Recommend against it in campaign #1: we have no baseline, and trademark bidding adds legal/policy noise to a test that should be measuring *our* message, not *their* brand equity.

## Differentiation recommendation

**Position Āagman as the proof-and-execution platform, not another analysis tool.**

The category conversation is stuck at "AI tells you things about stocks" (Tradomate: screeners; Draconic: second opinions). Nobody is claiming the full loop: **describe an idea in your language → backtest it → paper trade it → deploy it with institutional order types through your own broker — with you approving every step, under SEBI registration.**

Recommended angle hierarchy for the first Google Ads test:

1. **Lead:** Proof-first ("Backtest before you bet" territory — empty in the auction) + execution depth (TWAP/VWAP, multi-leg, MIT/LIT). Both are safe-to-claim today.
2. **Support:** SEBI/NSE credentials as the trust layer in descriptions and sitelinks — answers the objection Draconic spends 60% of its copy dancing around.
3. **Offer:** "Free in beta, no card — early users keep legacy rates forever." More specific and more durable than the category's generic "free."
4. **Test, don't lead:** Multi-language creative (Hinglish headlines on Search) as a separate ad group — high upside, zero competitor presence, but unproven CTR in this auction.

**Decision dependencies flagged from the brief** (these change which gaps matter): if the primary audience turns out to be *investors* rather than F&O traders, gap #6 (MF/investor workflows) jumps to lead position and the F&O-saturated crowded angles become irrelevant rather than contested. Budget, surface mix, and a numeric signup target remain undefined and will constrain how many of these angles we can test cleanly in parallel.

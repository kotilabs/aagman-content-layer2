# Raw Messaging Angles — Āagman Waitlist Campaign

Brainstorm only, unfiltered. Each angle maps to a verified capability; viability against demand data and policy is deliberately deferred.

1. **Angle:** "Prove it before you trade it" — every strategy gets backtested on historical data before a single rupee moves. | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §4.1 (Idea → Backtest → Paper → Live workflow), §8.1 (look-ahead-safe backtest mechanics), §14 pillar 2.

2. **Angle:** "Paper trade with live prices, zero capital" — promote any backtested strategy to real-time paper trading before going live. | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §4.1 step 4; caveat: §16 marks paper trading "Partially" safe — describe as workflow, verify live status before strong standalone claims.

3. **Angle:** "Backtest a strategy in one sentence" — describe the idea in plain language ("EMA crossover on RELIANCE, daily"), get a full report with Sharpe, drawdown, win rate. | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §6.1 (natural-language prompt grammar + example prompts), §8.2 (backtest report outputs).

4. **Angle:** "A stock screener that doesn't stop at screening" — screen 500+ stocks by technicals, fundamentals, or options metrics, then backtest the screen itself. | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §3 (Screener agent), §6.2 (screener metrics), §4.4 investor workflow step 2.

5. **Angle:** "Ask before you trade — and get the backtest with the answer" — an AI second opinion for F&O and equity decisions that comes with evidence attached. | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §14 pillar 1, §3 (Strategy Builder + Backtest Agent), §4.2 (score card + written reasoning).

6. **Angle:** "TWAP, VWAP, trailing stops, and 9 order types your broker app never shows you" — institutional-style execution through your existing broker. | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §7.1 (order types & algos), §7.2 (retail broker comparison table), §14 pillar 4.

7. **Angle:** "You approve every order — your capital never leaves your broker." | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §1 ("You brief, they work, you approve"; not a broker), §14 pillar 8, §7.3 (relay architecture).

8. **Angle:** "SEBI-registered Investment Adviser, NSE-empanelled algo provider — compliance built in, not bolted on." | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §9.2 (INA000021951, NSE Whitebox empanelment, auditable algo IDs), §14 pillar 5.

9. **Angle:** "Whitebox, not black-box — every algo's logic is disclosed, tagged, and auditable." | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §9.2 (Whitebox algorithms, exchange-assigned unique algo IDs, audit logging §9.3).

10. **Angle:** "Invest in the language you think in" — prompt in Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Bengali, or Telugu. | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §11 (supported languages), §6.1 (Hinglish/Hindi example prompts), §14 pillar 6.

11. **Angle:** "Compare mutual funds like an analyst" — screen by returns, expense ratio, category, sector, and holdings ("which funds hold RELIANCE?"). | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §5 (Mutual Funds via AMFI codes), §6.2 (MF screener metrics), §6.1 (MF example prompts).

12. **Angle:** "Find quality compounders without a spreadsheet" — screen for high ROE/ROCE, low debt, steady growth in one line. | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §5 (equities quality-compounder screens), §6.2 (fundamental metrics incl. Piotroski, earnings quality), §14 pillar 3.

13. **Angle:** "No code, no Excel, no Python — describe the rule, deploy the strategy." | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §2 (no-code systematic users), §6.1 (prompt grammar replaces scripting), §14 pillar 7.

14. **Angle:** "Built-in brakes: kill switches, daily loss caps, and circuit breakers watch every deployment." | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §9.1 (four-layer kill switch, pre-trade risk checks, circuit breakers), §3 (Risk agent).

15. **Angle:** "Free while in beta, no credit card — early users lock legacy rates forever." | **Safe-claim reference:** SoT §10 (pricing & access); flagged in brief as requiring offer confirmation before use in copy.

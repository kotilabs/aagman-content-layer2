# Keyword Research Seed List — Aagman Waitlist Campaign

## Purpose
Step 1 (seed/hypothesis) of the keyword research process. Run this list through
Google Ads Keyword Planner ("Get search volume and forecasts", India targeting)
and overlay monthly search volume + CPC before finalizing the campaign keyword list.

## Methodology (how keywords are actually decided)
1. Seed list from product/customer language (this file).
2. Expansion via Keyword Planner, autocomplete, People Also Ask, related searches.
3. Data overlay: monthly volume, CPC, competition. Kill anything <50 monthly searches.
4. Intent classification: informational / commercial / transactional. Bid on commercial + transactional first.
5. Cluster survivors into tight ad groups.
6. Post-launch: search terms report is the final truth. Review weekly.

## Themes covered
- Backtest (validated by no-code systematic trader persona)
- Algo/automation (validated by Draconic's category)
- Options (validated by F&O trader persona + Sensibull/Opstra market)
- Screener (validated by Tradomate's sustained spend + SEO moat)
- AI trading (category-level, Draconic's positioning)
- Trust/regulatory (our differentiator)
- Competitor brand terms (conquesting test)

## Notes
- "india" suffix kept on a few terms only — real searchers rarely add it;
  geo-targeting handles country. Instrument terms (NIFTY/NSE/BANKNIFTY) do the localizing.
- Competitor brand terms are for a small capped conquesting test, not the core list.
- Vendor-speak terms (4 leg options, multi leg options execution) were removed in v2
  of the campaign CSV after review — nobody searches feature-speak.

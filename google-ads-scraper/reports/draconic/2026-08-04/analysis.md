# Draconic.ai — Google Ads Inventory Analysis

**Snapshot:** 67 ads | ~90% image, ~10% video | Split roughly 55/45 between Display and YouTube | Campaign runs in distinct waves (Mar 2–8, Mar 12–22, Mar 27–May 21, May 21–Jun 8) — suggesting weekly creative sprints with full refreshes.

---

## 1. Overarching Themes

- **"Second opinion / ask before you trade"** is the dominant positioning: *"AI Second Opinion for F&O," "Ask AI Before Every Trade," "F&O Setup? Ask First."* Frames the product as validation, not automation.
- **Anti-bot disclaimer as a feature** — "Not a trading bot. No signals, no tips" appears in nearly every ad. Unusually defensive repetition; likely both a compliance shield (SEBI-adjacent risk) and a differentiation play.
- **Institutional democratization:** *"Think Like an FII Desk," "What prop desks see in seconds takes retail traders hours," "See What FIIs Positioned Today."* Retail-vs-institution gap is the core emotional hook.
- **Tool consolidation:** *"Stop switching between TradingView, Sensibull, and 3 news tabs," "Replace 3 tools," "One screen."*
- **Fear/stat-based urgency (sparingly used):** *"91% of F&O traders lose."*

## 2. Copy Patterns

- **Headlines:** Short, punchy, insight-forward ("What Your Nifty Chart Hides," "OI Tells You More Than Price," "Price is the last thing to move"). Search-style units use keyword-stacked dual headlines ("AI Stock Analysis - AI for Indian Stocks") — clearly SEO-intent harvesting.
- **Body:** Dense with trader jargon — OI, PCR, Greeks, IV, flow, Nifty/BankNifty, NSE/BSE. Signals a sophisticated F&O audience, not beginners (despite one "New to trading?" variant).
- **Free-offer scaffolding:** "Free signup, no card" / "No KYC. 2 Minutes" / "3 Free Analyses Daily" appears in ~70% of ads. Friction removal is the primary conversion lever.
- **CTAs:** "Sign Up" and "Open" (YouTube), "Free to Start" and "Visit Site" (Display). Search-style units use pill-button sitelink CTAs ("How It Works," "Pricing," "Free Trial").
- **Brand voice drift:** Tagline *"The hunt demands precision. Ask sharper. Trade better."* appears early (March) then disappears — possibly deprioritized.

## 3. Visual Style

Two distinct systems running in parallel:

1. **Display / search-style:** Minimalist white backgrounds, blue headlines, grey body, orange dragon logo. Text-heavy, RSA-rendered look. Functional, low production value.
2. **YouTube / brand:** Warm beige/cream backgrounds, dark brown serif typography, dark brown CTA bars — a deliberately "premium editorial" aesthetic unusual for fintech. Imagery: hands with pens over charts, silhouettes at multi-monitor desks, glowing figures behind curtains, golden key/lock.

**Notable inconsistency:** Orange brand icon appears in at least 3 variants (dragon silhouette, abstract vertical lines, pixelated grid) — brand identity isn't locked.

## 4. Surface/Channel Strategy

- **Display:** RSA-style units with multiple URL paths (/ai-trading, /ai/analysis, /trading/analysis, /intra_market/traders, /llm/trading) — indicating keyword/audience segmentation by use case (intraday, options sellers, scalpers, crypto).
- **YouTube:** Mix of static image units and ~6 video assets. Video is underutilized relative to the brand investment.
- **Competitor conquesting:** Direct callouts of Dhan, Zerodha, Sahi, TradingView, Sensibull, Nansen — aggressive comparison targeting.
- **Segment expansion test:** Crypto-themed ads appear briefly (Mar 2–8) then vanish — likely tested and killed in favor of core Indian F&O focus. May wave creatives pivot to "Built for Indian Traders" messaging — a successful localization bet.

## 5. Notable Trends & Actions

| Observation | Implication / Action |
|---|---|
| ~60% of copy repeats "Not a trading bot" | Trust barrier is the #1 objection. Consider testing *positive* framing ("You decide every trade") vs. negative disclaimers to see which converts. |
| Distinct creative waves with no overlaps | Structured A/B testing cadence — good. But no evergreen winners are being retained; consider keeping top performers running alongside tests. |
| "Built for Indian Traders" only in May–June wave | Localization angle is new and repeated across all recent ads — likely an early winner. Double down. |
| "3 Free Analyses Daily" appears once | Usage-capped free tier is a concrete, testable hook — worth promoting over generic "Free." |
| Brand/logo inconsistency + tagline dropped | Consolidate brand system before scaling spend. |
| Video only ~10% of inventory on a video-heavy surface mix | Produce more video; statics on YouTube underperform. |
| Some ads claim "It executes" / "AI That Trades for You" while others say "Not a bot" | **Messaging contradiction** — "AI analysis only — you make the call" vs. "agentic execution." Fix this; it risks both compliance exposure and user confusion. |

**Bottom line:** A disciplined, fast-iterating performance program with strong audience targeting and friction-free offers, anchored on "AI second opinion for Indian F&O traders." Biggest fixes: resolve the bot/execution contradiction, lock brand identity, and scale video.

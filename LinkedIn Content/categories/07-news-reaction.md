# Card 7 — News Reaction

## Reader outcome
The reader understands what changed AND what it means for them or for the writer — quickly. Pure restating of news is the failure this card exists to prevent.

## Available moves
Pick per event; most good news posts use only two or three.

- **Reader-first opening** — name who this affects before the event. Use when the mechanics affect a definable reader. Skip when the event itself is the drama.
- **Plain restatement with date** — what happened, when, in one or two sentences. Almost always needed somewhere early.
- **Mechanics chunks** — the operational changes as scannable blocks with real numbers. Use when readers must act on the change.
- **Payoff marker** — a short line flagging the part that matters most. Use in longer posts.
- **Action step close** — the exact thing to check or do. Use when there IS something to do; if there isn't, don't invent one.
- **Verdict close** — the writer's judgment, on the record. Use when the post's payload is the view, not the mechanics.
- **Inform-then-opine hybrid** — mechanics first, then the writer's take, clearly separated. Legal and often the right shape for a founder with a real position: the news earns attention, the take earns respect.

## Failure modes
- Restating the news with emotion and adding nothing.
- Sending readers elsewhere for the value.
- Benefit-dumps with no payoff turn.
- Stale news — if it's old, it's not news reaction.
- Forcing utility when the take is the point, or forcing a verdict when the mechanics are the point. Let the take decide the shape.

## Length prior
105–330 words (median ~260). Size to the mechanics; a one-line hot take works only when the take is exceptional.

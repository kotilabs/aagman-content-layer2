# Card 14 — Humor

## Reader outcome
The reader laughs, shares, and thinks a little more of the account's personality. Seasoning, never the meal.

## Available moves
- **Deadpan factual opening** — hyper-specific details stated with total seriousness. Specificity is what makes absurdity land.
- **Stepwise escalation** — each line raises the stakes one notch, straight face kept.
- **Mock-serious moral** — mimic earnest hustle-culture wisdom; the parody must be indistinguishable from sincerity until this point.
- **Punchline-last** — the reveal is the final line, and nothing may follow it. Non-negotiable: no explanation, no CTA, no earnest addendum after the joke.

## Failure modes
- Explaining the joke.
- Quips attached to topics instead of structural parody.
- Humor needing niche context to land.
- The mushy middle length.

## Length prior
Under ~65 words (median ~35), or fully commit to a ~190-word prank. Nothing between.

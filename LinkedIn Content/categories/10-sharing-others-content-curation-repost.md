# Card 10 — Sharing Others' Content (Curation/Repost)

## Reader outcome
The reader discovers something worth their time AND learns why this specific person is amplifying it.

## Available moves
- **Result-first opening** — the shared thing's most interesting fact, immediately. Never "excited to share."
- **One-line plain definition** — what it is, for someone smart with zero context.
- **The sharer's stake** — why YOU are amplifying this: what you witnessed, what you know, what your connection is. This is the load-bearing move; without it the post is unpaid advertising for someone else.
- **Reader pivot** — what they can do with it.
- **Door close** — a way in, or a forward-looking line.

## Failure modes
- Applause-only commentary.
- Vague teaser with no reveal.
- Filling a content gap — sharing because you needed a post.

## Length prior
40–175 words (median ~85). The shared content carries the weight; frame it, don't compete with it.

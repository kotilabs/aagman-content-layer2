# Card 11 — Gratitude / Shoutout

## Reader outcome
The reader meets one remarkable person and feels something real — generated by facts, never by adjectives.

## Available moves
- **Protagonist-first opening** — name them (or the relationship) plus one extraordinary specific fact. The writer is the witness, never the hero.
- **Parallel proof beats** — several short lines of the same grammatical shape, each a concrete thing they did or gave up. Repetition is the emotional device.
- **Universalize** — zoom out to what people like them mean.
- **Reciprocal close** — if a genuine mutual-bet twist exists, take it; it's the strongest closer here. Understated, one line.

## Failure modes
- Self-congratulation in gratitude's clothing (your award, your milestone, "humbled and honored").
- Adjective stacks instead of facts.
- Multiple people stacked into one post — one story, one person.

## Length prior
40–200 words (median ~80). Emotion compresses; a sprawling tribute stops feeling like one.

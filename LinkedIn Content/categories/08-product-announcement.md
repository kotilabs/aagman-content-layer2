# Card 8 — Product Announcement

## Reader outcome
The reader knows what now exists, what was broken before it, and whether it's for them — in under half a minute.

## Available moves
- **Name-it-first opening** — the thing, in line one. This space is the exception where directness beats storytelling; story-first announcements measurably underperform.
- **Broken before-state** — what the world was like, concretely, before this existed. Two or three specific sentences.
- **The turn line** — one short line, alone in its paragraph, marking that the before-state no longer holds.
- **Payoff triad** — three concrete things now true or now gone, parallel structure.
- **Exclusivity line** — telling some readers this isn't for them. Use as a trust device when true.
- **Direct invitation close** — short, specific, inline.

## Failure modes
- Bundling several features into one post — one idea per announcement.
- Teasers for things that don't exist yet.
- Marketing hedges ("potentially", "definitely worth exploring").
- Deflecting the CTA away from the post.

## Length prior
40–180 words (median ~110). Compression is respect here — the reader arrived knowing you're selling.

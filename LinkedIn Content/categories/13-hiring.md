# Card 13 — Hiring

## Reader outcome
The right reader thinks "I want to work for this person" — the post sells the manager, not the job description.

## Available moves
- **One-line opening** — hiring, role, company. No preamble.
- **Credibility line** — the single strongest reason the company is worth joining, compressed.
- **Identity list** — traits of the person (how they think, what they care about), not tasks, not credentials. Load-bearing: task lists attract applicants; identity attracts believers.
- **Upside line** — what the person joining now gets that no one gets later.
- **Frictionless close** — details in one obvious place; say what's NOT required.
- **News piggyback variant** — tie the opening to a current event affecting exactly the people you want. Use when such an event exists.

## Failure modes
- Pasted JD boilerplate and credential-gating.
- Corporate cheer closers.
- Hashtag stuffing and decoration.
- Length — every extra boilerplate paragraph measurably costs.

## Length prior
55–230 words (median ~135; the very best sit under 100).

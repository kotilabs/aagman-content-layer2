# Card 2 — Social Critique

## Reader outcome
The reader sees a system they live inside with new eyes, and gets a name or frame for something they had only felt.

## Available moves
- **Flat big claim opening** — a confident, falsifiable-feeling claim about the world, ideally with a time horizon or named group. Use when you hold the conviction fully. Skip — and reconsider posting — if you need to hedge it.
- **Name the phenomenon** — coin or cite a label for the thing. Use when the critique has a repeatable pattern worth carrying away.
- **Scene montage** — several witnessed moments, each landing on the same repeated point; the repetition is the argument. Use when you have real observed scenes.
- **Data ladder** — a numbered sequence of escalating facts. Use when numbers, not scenes, are your evidence.
- **Blast-radius widening** — show who is complicit and who pays; implicate systems, never individuals.
- **Sting close** — a prescription or quiet aphorism. Never end on a question or a CTA.

## Failure modes
- Hedging ("evidence suggests") — conviction-free critique performs worse than silence.
- Niche grievance — if the average reader doesn't live inside the topic, it isn't critique, it's industry complaining.
- Ranting without accumulation — outrage needs structure or it's noise.

## Length prior
110–430 words (median ~200). This is the one space where length helps — arguments need room to accumulate.

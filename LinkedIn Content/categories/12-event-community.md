# Card 12 — Event / Community

## Reader outcome
The reader feels they missed something worth attending — experience, not logistics.

## Available moves
- **Time-anchored opening** — when it happened, plainly, first person. Strongest version includes an expectation gap: expected one thing, got another.
- **Unpolished emotion** — the candid reaction, underproduced on purpose.
- **One-line belief** — why you gather these people, compressed to an aphorism. This is what elevates a diary entry into a post.
- **Soft forward pointer** — a next date or quiet link, low pressure.
- **Thank by name** — real names of real people who were there.

## Failure modes
- Poster mode: agendas, logistics, calendar instructions.
- Location-only openings with no tension.
- A pitch smuggled into the recap.
- Length — if there's one true moment and one belief, that's the whole post.

## Length prior
35–235 words (median ~180; many winners are 2–5 lines). Never long.

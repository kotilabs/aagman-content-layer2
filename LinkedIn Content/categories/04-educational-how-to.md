# Card 4 — Educational / How-To

## Reader outcome
The reader can do something today they couldn't do yesterday, and can check that it works.

## Available moves
- **Audience callout opening** — name exactly who this is for. Use when the lesson has a definable beneficiary. Skip when the lesson is universal.
- **Ignorance gap** — reveal the hidden layer in something the reader already handles. Use when there's a genuine unknown-known; never manufacture fake secrets.
- **Numbered mechanics** — chunked steps, each with a label and a concrete number or rule. Use for anything procedural.
- **Homework close** — a specific action completable in minutes. Use almost always; teaching without a task evaporates.

## Failure modes
- Abstract principle with no mechanics.
- No named audience — a lecture to everyone reaches no one.
- No action step at the end.
- Vague advice ("do your research", "stay disciplined") — the floor of this space.

## Length prior
70–330 words (median ~285). Density is the requirement; every line must carry a fact or a step.

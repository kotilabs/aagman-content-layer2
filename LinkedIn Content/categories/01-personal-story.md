# Card 1 — Personal Story

## Reader outcome
The reader feels they know the person behind the account a little better, and carries away one principle drawn from a real life — not advice, a lived result.

## Available moves
Use whichever serve THIS material. Skip the rest.

- **Gap opening** — place two states of a life side by side (then/now, expectation/reality). Use when the story has a genuine before/after. Skip when the story is a single moment, not a transition.
- **Objection pre-emption** — name the reader's likely objection and disarm it. Use when the story's central choice looks wrong on its face. Skip when nothing in the story needs defending.
- **Compressed-time beats** — a sequence of short escalating lines that carry years in seconds. Use when the arc spans a long period. Skip for single-scene stories.
- **Reframe** — late in the post, the central fact turns out to mean something other than what it literally was. Use when the story has that double-meaning honestly available. Never manufacture one.
- **Hand-off close** — end by handing the reader the principle, not by summarizing your feelings. Use almost always.
- **Caption mode** — when a real photo carries the meaning, one line of text is the whole post. Use only with a genuine photo.

## Failure modes
- Corporate announcement tone on personal material.
- Telling someone else's story as if it were yours.
- Vulnerability without specifics — reads as fishing.
- The 400-word mushy middle: too long to be a moment, too thin to be an arc.

## Length prior
Bimodal: under ~15 words (caption mode) or 150–290 words (full arc). Size to the story, not the range.

# Card 9 — Content Promotion

## Reader outcome
The reader gets something of value from the post itself, and the promoted content becomes an offer of more — never a prerequisite.

## Available moves
- **Story-first opening** — tell the best story or finding inside the content, standalone. The core rule of this space: summarize and it's an ad; narrate and it's content.
- **Withheld-identity loop** — the outcome before the name; the gap pulls the reader through. Use when the subject has a dramatic arc.
- **Substance teasers** — each previewed item contains the actual insight or number, not a topic label.
- **Late bridge** — mention the content only after the post has delivered.
- **Single short CTA close.**

## Failure modes
- Asking for engagement before delivering value.
- Topic-list teasers with no narrative.
- Centering the host instead of the subject.
- Template fatigue — identical promo structures posted weekly get trained into invisibility.

## Length prior
95–300 words (median ~255). Promo needs a full arc to work; under ~90 words it's a teaser, and teasers underperform.

# Card 5 — Market / Business Analysis

## Reader outcome
The reader's model of a company, market, or industry is updated in a way they couldn't have gotten from the news.

## Available moves
- **Inversion opening** — a claim that contradicts the reader's assumption, flatly. Use when you genuinely hold the counterintuitive finding. If your finding isn't counterintuitive, this is not the shape — or not a post.
- **Independent fact blocks** — one fact, one number per paragraph, no transitions. Use for evidence-dense analysis.
- **Self-deconstruction** — take apart your own most impressive figure. Use whenever a number looks too good; this is the trust engine.
- **Verdict close** — project forward, on the record. Never end asking the audience what they think.

## Failure modes
- Summarizing known information — commoditized content.
- Hype openings instead of inversions.
- Data dumps with no thesis.
- Previewing structure ("I'll explain in three layers") instead of just explaining.

## Length prior
165–390 words (median ~230). Under ~100 words an analysis reads as an unsupported claim; over ~400 it should have been an article.

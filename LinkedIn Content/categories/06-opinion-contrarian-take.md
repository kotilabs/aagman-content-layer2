# Card 6 — Opinion / Contrarian Take

## Reader outcome
The reader reconsiders something they took for granted, and respects the writer more even if they disagree.

## Available moves
- **Flat observation opening** — the position stated plainly, cold open, present tense. The "wait, what?" is the hook.
- **Generalization** — show it's not about one thing but a whole category of things.
- **Self-implication** — include yourself in the indictment; admit your luck, doubt, or complicity. This is what separates perspective from hot take. Near-mandatory: contrarianism without it reads as arrogance.
- **Aphorism close** — the position distilled to one quotable line.

## Failure modes
- Imperative-lecture openings ("Stop doing X") — they invite resistance; observations invite agreement.
- Outrage register — this genre runs on calm, amused detachment.
- Length creep — a take that needs 500 words is an essay; write a critique instead.
- No personal stake — opinions that cost the author nothing are worth nothing.

## Length prior
10–140 words (median ~41). The take must fit in a breath.

## Universal
- STRONG — First-person openings outperform. Median score 131 for "I did/saw X"
  hooks vs 91 for generic statements and 75 for question-openers. Opening with a
  question was the weakest hook pattern measured.
- STRONG — One sentence per line with blank lines between beats: near-universal
  in top-quartile posts, absent in bottom-quartile.
- STRONG — Hashtag walls/blocks correlate with the bottom half of every category.
- STRONG — Promotion signals suppress engagement: "link in comments" deflections,
  corporate emotion-words ("thrilled, honored, humbled"), explicit excitement
  announcements. The more a post reads as a gift to the reader, the better.
- STRONG — Specificity (concrete numbers, dates, names) co-occurs with
  outperformance in every category.
- STRONG — Questions work as closers, not openers.
- STRONG — Winning posts contain a turn: the fact established early gets
  redefined or deconstructed mid/late post. Posts that end where they started
  land flat.
- DIRECTIONAL — Emoji restraint: 0–1 per post in winners, at the emotional beat,
  never as bullets or decoration.
- DIRECTIONAL — Bimodal length: very short and fully-developed posts both beat
  the half-essay middle.
- STRONG — Personal stake beats topical coverage. Across all 11 creators,
  posts where the author had skin in the content beat baseline ~80% of the time.
- DIRECTIONAL — Engagement-bait CTAs ("comment X", "what do you think?")
  underperform declarative closes.

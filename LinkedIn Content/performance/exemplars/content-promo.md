## Exemplar 1 (score 444, creator baseline = 100)

This CA started her career with a monthly salary of just ₹1,800.

Today, she manages an AUM of ₹35,000 crore and leads a team of 750 members.

She acquired a 96% stake in her firm by putting her own home on the line to complete a management buyout.

On my podcast, we discussed her incredible journey from being a Gujarati medium student to becoming the owner of one of India's most respected financial firms.

-> During her CA Inter exams she appeared for all 8 papers despite preparing for only 4 because her father insisted the exam fees should not go to waste.

-> After joining her current firm in 1999 she spent 15 days every month traveling across the globe to build the institutional brokerage business from scratch.

-> Between 2003 and 2007 she scaled the firm's brokerage revenue from ₹1 crore to over ₹100 crore through high-conviction research sales.

-> Instead of taking her massive earnings as a cash bonus she chose to convert her performance into equity to become a shareholder in the firm.

-> In 2022 she took the ultimate risk by using structured finance and personal guarantees to buy out the remaining promoters and take her stake to 96%.

-> She was a 3-time winner of the best salesperson award in India while traveling the world for research without ever compromising on her core values.

-> She believes that no background is a barrier to success if you focus your energy on adding genuine value rather than making excuses.

She is none other than Amisha Vora, Chairperson and MD of PL Capital Group (Prabhudas Lilladher).

## Exemplar 2 (score 406, creator baseline = 100)

The day he became a CA, he told his father:

“Dad, please stop selling vegetables. You have been doing this for 40 years now. I want you to retire!”

His dad used to earn ₹200 per day by selling vegetables in Dadar from 4 am. 

The day he became a CA, he started working in Oman, and got a job of ₹1.5L/month. 

After that, he bough a ₹1 Crore house for his parents in Thane, so that they could live a comfortable life. His parents used to live in a small 150 square feet chawl in Vikhroli in Mumbai. 

He is none other than CA Abhishek R. Vaishya - YT. 

We have documented his entire journey, right from going to the vegetables supermarket in Dadar at 5 am in the morning (where they met their old friends), to going at his old 150 sq ft. Chawl in Vikhroli, to going at his new house in Thane - all in one super inspiring video that will give you goosebumps! 

Full video out on my YouTube channel! 

When I asked him about his advice for everyone, Abhishek said:

“Just believe in yourself, and stay focused to your goals. Some people might take 1 year, some will take 10 years but everyone will achieve it if you are focused!”

Share this with all your CA friends, and show them the power of this degree - it can literally change someone’s life!

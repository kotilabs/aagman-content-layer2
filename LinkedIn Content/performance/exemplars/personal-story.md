## Exemplar 3 (score 1656, creator baseline = 100)

I cleared CA with an AIR 5.

But I failed ACCA.

I failed not because I wasn't prepared, or because I was nervous.

I failed because I remembered the examination timing incorrectly, and the window closed before I could finish.

Three months later, I retook it and secured World Rank 3.

That failure taught me: knowledge is useless if you do not respect time.

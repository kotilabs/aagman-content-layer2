## Exemplar 3 (score 321, creator baseline = 100)

Given my passion for all things health, I keep getting into all sorts of activities that I probably shouldn't, considering my age. Yesterday, the peakst8 team and I were trying a new hybrid fitness format called No Parking. It wasn’t as challenging as Hyrox, but it definitely wasn’t a cakewalk either.

My heart rate shot up quickly with push-ups, rowing, sit-ups, and mountain climbers, while my teammate Deepak did all the activities that involved running😄

I'm excited to see how everyone else feels about this new format. We're taking this event live in Delhi on August 23rd with one8world.

The idea here is to inspire more people to start their fitness journeys.

The belief is simple: more people will start getting fit if fitness is fun. That’s why we’re creating multiple fitness IPs, all built around participation.

The bet is that people start by taking part in these events, enjoy themselves, and hopefully that motivates them to begin their own fitness journeys.

If you’ve been meaning to get fit, check out one of the Peakst8 events (link in comments).

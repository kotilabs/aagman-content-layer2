## Exemplar 1 (score 917, creator baseline = 100)

30 years from now India will be an example of how social media ruined a country.

If everyone is sick, we no longer call it a disease. One such disease that's widespread in India today is watching reels for hours and hours. India has cheap and fast data. People have a lot of time and there are millions of unemployed youth so what are they doing? Doom scrolling.

You see around, every single person is scrolling reels. People are scrolling while commuting in bus, train, cabs. They scroll just after waking up and just before sleeping, while eating and even during traffic signal. You'll see people scrolling in office and even when they are with friends and family.

Even children today are scrolling. They won't have food without reels or shorts. Imagine how are we raising our upcoming generation? This should be treated as an epidemic and the least our government can do is to ban social media for kids just like Australia did a while ago.

## Exemplar 2 (score 556, creator baseline = 100)

I live in Bengaluru. I leave for badminton every day at 6:40 am on my bike. But I booked an auto to the court today. It is Indiranagar, auto arrived within minutes but I was still getting ready. To my surprise, driver neither called me nor he was angry when I went downstairs. He was busy scrolling reels.

I gave my OTP and the ride started. There is a signal near to my house. It was green but only 4 seconds were left. Driver accelerated but the timer ran out. We were stuck at the signal for next 90 seconds. To my left stopped a Tata Nexon. The lady driving must be in her early 30s. She turned off her engine, picked her phone and started scrolling reels.

I reached court on time but my friend was yet to come. I went to reception to ask about the court number. An old uncle who was of my father's age was sitting there. He didn't respond in the first time. He had held his phone to his eye level and was busy scrolling reels.

My court number was 3. It was 6:55 am. This court was probably booked by 5 people from 6 to 7 am. 4 were playing doubles and the 5th guy was waiting. He wasn't bothered about watching the game, he was busy scrolling reels.

Reels have quietly taken over the world.

## Exemplar 3 (score 435, creator baseline = 100)

India has a new class called the Laptop Class... and if you're part of this class, I'll tell you why you are absolutely cooked...

Out of 140+ crore people in India, 100 crore vote, but only 4 crore pay taxes...

Which means, if you are someone in an Indian metro, who earns between Rs 15 lakh to Rs 75 lakh, and have a white collar job, you fall in this bracket...

You are less than 3% of India's total population that pays taxes, but you barely make a dent in the voter base... so all the policies that the govt makes for subsidies to the poor don't reach you...

And all the deductions for business owners, you aren't eligible for...

But this is just the tip of the iceberg... 

The real problem starts when you look at how the country's economic data impacts you...

1/ Currently, India's unemployment rate is at the highest it has been in a decade... 

The IT sector, which used to be one of the largest mass recruiters like Infosys, TCS, Wipro are on a hiring freeze... and out of 1.5 Million engineering graduates that come out every year, more than 80% graduate without a job at hand...

But this problem has now moved beyond freshers...

People who are in middle level management in their 40s have realised that they are part of corporate bloat... 

All the growth in IT jobs are currently coming from roles in AI/ML, Big Data, and Cybersecurity... and if you're not part of these domains, your only focus is to maintain your job because no other company will you pay you your current pay if you try switching...

So the focus is to save your job, and not be fired because of AI and automation.

2/ India's net household savings rate is at ~5%, which is the lowest in the past 5 decades... but where are people's savings eroding? Is it in housing?

No!

Because ~60% of financial loans are now for consumption and not to buy a house...

So EMIs, cars, phones, credit cards... basically, social media usage over the past 8 years has just skyrocketed people's aspirations while swaying them away from actual skillbuilding or learning to stay competitive in the workspace...

Which means, while you have job insecurity and don't know if you'll continue to earn more in the future, there is no stopping of taking loans that are based on future repayments...

3/ And to top this, comes the craze for investing and making money in the stock market, in real estate, in gold, in silver, in F&O trading...

Where people are resorting to F&O trading, not realising more than 90% of traders lose on an average Rs 1.1 lakh annually... and want to be part of the next boom, when the country's stock market hasn't given returns in over 18 months, real estate is getting into oversupply, and gold so expensive, you're afraid it will fall.

With all of this, we want to blame the war, the oil, the taxes, the policy, and everything else...

India's biggest economic issue right now is not the war, or oil... but an unskilled population that is too distracted by news and social media.

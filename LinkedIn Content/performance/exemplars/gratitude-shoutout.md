## Exemplar 1 (score 662, creator baseline = 100)

My dad bought me an iPhone 5s in my Grade 11 whereas he was using a Nokia mobile.

My dad sent me to the best school in Nashik that had fees of ₹1L per annum, but he studied in a normal school. 

My dad bought a touch screen laptop for me when I cleared CA Inter but he doesn’t even know how to use a laptop. 

My dad bought me a new branded T-shirt whenever we used to go for shopping but he never bought anything for himself when we went out.

That’s what every father is meant to be - always selfless, caring and thinking about you more than himself. 

They will never say “I love you” to you, but every action of theirs will show unconditional love! 

Happy Father’s Day to the best father in the world! Although I couldn’t say this to you as well, so here I say “I love you the most”!

## Exemplar 1 (score 530, creator baseline = 100)

If you're a fresher earning under ₹1 lakh a month, you should read this - 


Last year the government launched a scheme called PM Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana.

The scheme has two parts. One is for first-time employees basically freshers in their first job, whose EPF is getting deducted.
 
They will get up to ₹15,000 from the government.

To qualify: 
→ Your joining date falls between 1 Aug 2025 and 31 July 2027
→ Your gross salary is under ₹1 lakh a month

The money will be credited in two installments-

First one is up to ₹7,500

Work for continuous 6 months in the same company, then based on your EPF wage (amount on which EPF is calculated) money will be credited in your bank account 

Similar for 2nd installment (up to ₹7500) work in the same company for 12 months, complete a small financial literacy course and done!

Now the best part? 

You don't have to apply anywhere, or fill a form just go to the EPFO portal and open the "PMVBRY" tab. If you're eligible, it'll show right there, and you can track your status from the same page.

You have to check few things from you end - 
→ Your UAN should be KYC-verified
→ Your Aadhaar should be linked to your UAN
→ Your bank account should be Aadhaar-seeded (You can do this on NPCI website)

Some people on Reddit said they got the money, some didn't. So just make sure everything is set up right from your end and if you eligible you will get it:)

## Exemplar 2 (score 300, creator baseline = 100)

Good news for salaried people!!

For more details:

• These changes were announced in October 2025, but the government notified them only a few days ago. They are effective from 1st July.

• To use these new features, the EPFO portal is being updated. As per the latest update, it is expected to go live from 3rd July.

• Once the portal is updated, the new withdrawal limits and other changes will start applying.

• One more important change: the withdrawal limit for each reason has been reset. For example, if you had already withdrawn twice for marriage earlier, you don’t have only 3 attempts left. From 1st July, your count starts fresh and you can again make up to 5 withdrawals for marriage (subject to EPF rules). The same applies to other eligible withdrawal reasons.

• If you leave your job before completing 12 months of EPF membership, you can now make a partial withdrawal, up to your eligible EPF balance.

• You can also withdraw 100% of your EPF in certain situations:

* If you permanently move abroad or take up employment abroad.
* If you are laid off.
* On retirement at the age of 55.
